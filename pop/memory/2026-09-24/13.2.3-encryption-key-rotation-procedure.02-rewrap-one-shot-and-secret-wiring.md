# Rewrap core, profile-gated one-shot and secret wiring

`src/security/key-rotation.ts` holds the testable rewrap logic (injected store, no Docker/exit); `rotate-encryption-keys.mjs` is the thin secrets wrapper. Updates are conditional on ciphertext observed, idempotent, never a webhook. `runtime.mjs`/`compose.yaml`/installer stage two optional previous-key secrets behind an `up`-excluded profile. 2 out-of-`owns` deviations fixed by coordinator: `Dockerfile`, `db-contract-check.mjs`.
