# Schema, migration history and pinned baseline rebased to V2-only

`schema.prisma` lost the four V1-only tables and the `provider_order.payment_link_order_id` column. The four V1-origin migrations (`20260721020000_payment_links`, `20260721030000_owner_isolation_checkout_policy`, `20260721040000_payment_link_orders`, `20260721050000_public_checkout_attempts`) were folded into the rebase, and `prisma/migration-policy-baseline.json` re-pinned to the resulting 16-migration V2-only baseline under the explicit destructive authorization.
