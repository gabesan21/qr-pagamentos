# One production rule, startup gate, installer-derived allowance

`isAcceptableOperatorOrigin` alone decides; call sites untouched. `container/runtime.mjs` restates it (callback + file-backed `PUBLIC_ORIGIN`), fixing its old HTTPS-only callback check. `compose.yaml` declares the allowance `${…:-}`; install/update/restore derive and forward it, never writing `install/.env`.
