# Remodel administrator settings hub

Recomposed `src/app/admin/settings/page.tsx` and `src/app/admin/settings/settings-surface.tsx` onto the template visual language, adding `exchange-currencies-section.tsx`, `catalog-records-section.tsx`, `payment-settings-section.tsx`, `appearance-section.tsx`, `language-section.tsx`, and `confirm-toggle.tsx`. Reused existing POST routes (`/admin/settings/default-theme`, `/admin/exchange-currencies`) and preserved read-only/privileged-action boundaries. Updated `src/app/admin/admin-submit.tsx` and `src/app/admin/loading.tsx` for shared submit and loading states. Adjusted `src/app-shell/app-shell.css` to prevent top-bar overflow on narrow viewports. Updated administration and shared dictionaries for `pt-BR` and `en`. Added `tests/admin-settings.evidence.spec.ts` for visual/contract evidence across six themes, two locales, and 320/375/768/1440 widths.
