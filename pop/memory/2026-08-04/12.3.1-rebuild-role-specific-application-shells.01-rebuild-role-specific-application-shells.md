# Rebuild role-specific application shells

Expanded `AppShell` with a server-only frame: persistent 248px desktop rail, overlay mobile drawer, sticky top bar, account menu, native language form, and footer strip. `shell-navigation.tsx` remains the sole client boundary and imports no auth or business modules. Admin and merchant layouts supply five-entry role-bound navigation, and the `app-shell` dictionary covers both `pt-BR` and `en`.
