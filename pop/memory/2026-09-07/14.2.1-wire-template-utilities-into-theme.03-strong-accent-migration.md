# `accent` repointed to the strong role, consumers migrated

`--color-accent` moved from the pale `var(--accent)` (a `color.action.soft` alias) to `color.action.accent`, and the tint kept the template's own name, `accent-soft`. The four shadcn consumers that meant the tint — three `active:bg-accent` button variants and the checked field label — became `bg-accent-soft`, so their rendered color is unchanged. `src/app/not-found.tsx`, already authored in this vocabulary, was left untouched and now renders its `text-accent` icon in the strong accent.
