# Badge tone in a single owner

`StatusBadge` became a soft-tint pill with a `bg-current` dot and now also exports `ProviderStateBadge`, `LocalOutcomeBadge`, `LinkLifecycleBadge`, `AccountStateBadge` and `EntityStateBadge` over one 17-state tone map taken from the template. Each family owns a closed union and takes `labels` from the caller, so shared UI resolves tone and shape while the caller keeps language and no domain enum crosses the boundary. The public signature is unchanged: all 38 call sites compile untouched.
