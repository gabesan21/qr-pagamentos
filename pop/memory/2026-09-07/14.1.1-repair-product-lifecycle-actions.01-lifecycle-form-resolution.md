# Confirmations resolve the real form by id

Both `ConfirmDialog` handlers on the product detail called `requestSubmit()` on `useRef`s that were never attached to any element, so archive and activate/deactivate were unreachable from the UI. The refs are gone; a local `submitFormById` resolves `product-active-toggle` and `product-archive` through `document.getElementById` and submits them — the same mechanism already used by `catalog-submit` and `link-v2-actions`, so the catalog now has one resolution pattern instead of two. Both confirmations still gate their action.
