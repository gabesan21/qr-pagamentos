# `localAdditions`: a home for excluded sources

The uploader could not enter `inventory.json`'s `owners`: its template source is `excluded-unreachable-generated-ui`, and three checks fail closed if it becomes a reachable owner. A new `localAdditions` section records owner, public API, states and a one-line insufficiency instead; the validator enforces it and refuses collisions with `owners` or an exclusion. `owners` stays 20; the spec's "No upload component is admitted" became the local-addition rule.
