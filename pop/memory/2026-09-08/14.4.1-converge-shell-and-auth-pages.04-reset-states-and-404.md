# Reset states, on-page success and bilingual 404

Reset now composes the shared card and proves the two states the service can distinguish — missing token and rejected link — each with the generated `unavailable` illustration and a back-to-login action. The 404 stopped being pt-BR only through a new `not-found` dictionary domain registered in both locales.
