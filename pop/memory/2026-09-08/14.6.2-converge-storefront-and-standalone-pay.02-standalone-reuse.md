# The standalone payment yields to CheckoutPaymentView

Once a payment exists the form is gone and `CheckoutPaymentView` renders: no page-local tone map (`paymentTone`/`paymentCopy` deleted), no QR/terminal markup, refunded neutral via `ProviderStateBadge`. The unavailable view became `EmptyState`.

**Deviation:** `useCheckoutExperience` is welded to `/api/payment-links/[identifier]/**` and a customer-only body, so the amount-bearing attempt keeps a local phase under the same rule: edits only clear their validation flag, `startOver` is the only reset, an issued payment survives every keystroke.
