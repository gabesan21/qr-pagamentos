# Session-draft primitive promoted to `src/app/form-draft.tsx`

The catalog-only `catalog-draft.tsx` became the shared `form-draft.tsx` (`saveFormDraft`/`readFormDraft`/`clearFormDraft`/`hasFailureNotice`/`FormDraftGuard`) under the `qr-form-draft:` prefix, with the three catalog importers migrated and catalog behaviour unchanged. The guard now also captures textareas and selects and excludes file, password, checkbox, and radio inputs by type, so a comment body survives a failed native POST while a secret never reaches sessionStorage.
