# The determinism failure was contention, diagnosed not assumed

Round 1 hit the screenshot-determinism check (`design-system.evidence.spec.ts:482`) twice and proposed `verify: user`. The gate rejected it: the same hash pair twice is reproducibility, not rasterizer noise, and 14.3.4 and 14.4.4 passed that assertion in this sandbox. Round 2 found the cause — an orphaned `next-server` from round 1 still bound to port 4319, competing for CPU. Killed, isolated in a preflight, rerun detached: 48/48 captures clean. No Phase 14.5 component renders non-deterministically.
