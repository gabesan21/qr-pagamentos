# 14.2.3-mount-toasts-and-origin-locale-switch — Mount toasts and origin locale switch


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.2]]
- **Plano:** [[14.2.3-mount-toasts-and-origin-locale-switch.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.2.3-mount-toasts-and-origin-locale-switch.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.2.3-mount-toasts-and-origin-locale-switch.verify]]

## O quê

`ToastViewport` is mounted for every route; query-string outcome notices surface as template toasts (success auto-dismiss, sticky errors with retry where applicable) with an accessible non-JS fallback; the locale POST returns to any same-origin internal origin page, and the login/reset pages gain the template's compact language switcher.

## Por quê

The toast owner has zero production call sites and every notice is a redirect banner; the locale switch still bounces users to `/` and auth pages have no language control.
