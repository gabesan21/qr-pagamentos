## Contrato de execução

- **Re-grep first**, then drop these leaves: `auth-brand`, `auth-card__{tagline,caption,strip,swatch,footer}`, `auth-card__form--tight`, `auth-password-field{,__input,__toggle}`, `auth-forgot{,__link,__note}`, `auth-totp-actions`, `auth-mode-toggle`, `login-page`, `login-form`, `reset-password-page`, `reset-password-form`, `auth-page`.
- **The 900px block stays.** `.auth-card`, `.auth-card__panel`, `.auth-card__form` and `.auth-card__language` carry the split-card responsive contract through `@media (min-width: 900px)`. `check-design-tokens.mjs` sanctions that literal **only inside `globals.css`**; `min-[900px]:` in a `.tsx` is a raw-value violation and Tailwind cannot express the breakpoint through a `var()` (CSS forbids `var()` in a media query). So keep those four class names on their elements, migrate nothing that depends on them, and name them in your report as the exception F03 must document. Attempting to move them out is out of contract.
- `.auth-brand` is a descendant override reaching into `BrandIdentity`'s internal `brand-identity__mark`/`__name`. Swapping it for the `compact-role-lockup` variant would change the rendered `data-brand-identity` value — a component-semantics change, out of scope. Either express the remaining margin with a utility while keeping the mark sizing identical, or keep `.auth-brand` and **record it as a gap** with one line saying what a fix would need. Never change `BrandIdentity`'s API, variants or props.
- **No raw literal, no inline style.** Use `var(--space-*)`, `var(--auth-form-max)`, `var(--target-min-size)`, `var(--type-*)`, `var(--disabled-opacity)` inside arbitrary values; `50%` and `translate-y-1/2` are fine (the gate matches `px|rem|em|ch|#hex` only).
- Preserve every `id` (`login-form` is read by `login-form.test.tsx` through `getElementById`), every `name`, `type`, `autoComplete`, `aria-*`, `role` and the no-JS `<form>` submission path exactly.
- Test realignment is bounded to these lines: `login/page.test.tsx` 37, 38, 45 and `reset-password/page.test.tsx` 41, 42, 43, 73. Assertions on `auth-card__panel`/`auth-card__form` stay valid (those classes survive); replace only the ones whose class you removed, and prefer asserting a role/label/`id` over a utility string. Remove no assertion without replacing what it proved.
- Auth pages are pixel-evidence surfaces (`tests/login.evidence.spec.ts`, `artifacts/login`, `artifacts/reset-password`). Regeneration belongs to 14.7.2; do not run, edit or regenerate any artifact.
- Evidence commands, direct binaries only, never `pnpm <script>` and never a container: `./node_modules/.bin/tsc --noEmit`, `node scripts/check-design-tokens.mjs`, `./node_modules/.bin/eslint <changed files>`.
