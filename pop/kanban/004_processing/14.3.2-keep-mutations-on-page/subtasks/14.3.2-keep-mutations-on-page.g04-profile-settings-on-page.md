## O que entregar

1. **Password rotation.** `profile.changePassword` keeps revoking every session atomically inside the user lock — do not touch `src/auth/profile.ts`. After it returns, the route issues one fresh session for the same actor (`getSessionService().create`, or `createMfaVerified` when the actor's TOTP is active, so the recorded MFA fact is not silently downgraded), sets `qr_session` with the same cookie options the login route uses, and redirects to `/profile?password=changed`. Drop the `qr_locale` write and the locale resolve: they existed only to carry the locale into an anonymous `/login` landing that no longer happens. Failure keeps returning `/profile?password=failed` with the session untouched, and the opaque single failure shape is unchanged.
2. **Identity and TOTP.** Both already return to `/profile`; what is missing is the announcement. Resolve the closed notice sets as the page already does and render them through the toast bridge with a `<noscript>` `Alert` fallback, following the orders/links/catalog notice components. The TOTP notice is painted twice today — in `profile-management.tsx` and again in `totp-section.tsx`; exactly one site survives. Give the identity form a draft guard from the shared primitive so username and contact email survive a `conflict` or `failed` return.
3. **Nautt.** The three credential routes return to `/settings` with their closed notice but no section anchor, so the merchant lands at the top of a long page. Append `#settings-connection` on every branch, exactly as the storefront and checkout-policy routes already do for their sections. The Nautt notice is also painted twice — in `settings-surface.tsx` and again in `nautt-credential-surface.tsx`; one site survives, delivered through the toast bridge with its `<noscript>` fallback. **The API key is a secret: never write it to a draft**, so the credential form gets no guard.

The TOTP enrolment restructure, the manual-secret CopyField, the regenerate confirmation and the settings two-column styling are **not** this task.
