## O quê

Raise every interactive target below 44 px to the contract minimum (quantity steppers and remove button in `link-lines-editor.tsx`, merchant and admin period controls, any control the 15.4.1 sweep reveals); retire the dictionary debt — the 143 unreferenced keys recorded as F4 by 15.1.4 (confirming each by static and dynamic-access search), the dead key `merchantDashboardSalesEmpty`, and a plural form for `{count} products` — in both locales; and restore the two UI gates that drifted: refresh the parity obligations of `pay/[identifier]/loading.tsx` and `store/[slug]/pay/loading.tsx` changed by 15.3.3 (F5/C3 of 15.3.4) and limit the parity checker's semantic derivation to the tracked tree so the gitignored Prisma client can no longer be baked into `obligations.ndjson` (F2 of 15.1.4).

## Por quê

Fitts' 44 px law and the dictionary parity test are part of the design contract; the two gate defects make `frontend-parity:check` fail on every developer machine after `pnpm check` regardless of UI work.
