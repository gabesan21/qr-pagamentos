# 14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links — Converge merchant payment links


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.5]]
- **Plano:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.verify]]

## O quê

`/links`, `/links/new`, `/links/v2/[id]`, `/links/v2/[id]/edit`, `/links/v2/[id]/orders` and its order detail reproduce the template: directory with era/currency/date filters and the currency column plus merged legacy rows with a read-only modal; create form with radio-card composition and type, searchable product lines with steppers, unit prices and running total, localized descriptions for every link, expiration clear, sticky live preview and inline errors; detail with action row under the header, lifecycle guards (paid single-use never reactivates, expired only if reusable) with captions, real orders summary, unavailable-product badge; edit with locked structural fields and conditional new-version banner; breadcrumbs on the drill-down pages.

## Por quê

The merchant links audit rates create/edit low and the directory/detail medium-low: native selects instead of radio cards, no product search or totals, hardcoded orders summary, lifecycle rules absent in UI and legacy links rendered through the admin form.
