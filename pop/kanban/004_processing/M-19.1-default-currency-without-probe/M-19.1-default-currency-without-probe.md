# M-19.1-default-currency-without-probe — Automatic default currency without merchant probes

- **Origin:** [[pop/MODIFICATIONS|M-19]] — follow for the change request and status.
- **Plan:** [[M-19.1-default-currency-without-probe.plan]] — follow for the proposed implementation and acceptance criteria.

## What

Newly created merchant accounts automatically receive the system's first eligible administrator-configured active payment method as their default conversion currency. A merchant can choose another active administrator-configured method/currency and save without running a test; remove the merchant's Test pair action and its prerequisite from selection.

## Why

Today a new merchant starts with no persisted default currency and saving a chosen currency is blocked until its owner runs a provider quote probe, even though the administrator already registered the option.
