# 14.4.1-converge-shell-and-auth-pages — Converge shell and auth pages


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.4]]
- **Plano:** [[14.4.1-converge-shell-and-auth-pages.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.4.1-converge-shell-and-auth-pages.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.4.1-converge-shell-and-auth-pages.verify]]

## O quê

The authenticated shell and the shared access pages reproduce the template: route-derived top-bar title, rail/drawer switch at `lg` (1024px), rail footer with monogram + username + role pill and the `by Nautt Finance` caption, `View storefront` link for merchants with an enabled store, template nav labels; login with brand panel on the left (tagline, gradient strip, six swatches), forgot-password link, show/hide password, inline required errors, 6-cell TOTP input with paste/auto-submit and Back; reset page with distinct invalid/expired/used states, back-to-login, 12-128 hint with meter and client mismatch feedback, success on the same page; bilingual 404 authored in template utilities.

## Por quê

The shell/auth family audit rates fidelity low-medium: static top-bar title, 768px breakpoint, bare right-side brand panel, plain TOTP text box, collapsed reset states and a pt-BR-only 404 diverge from the template's designed journeys.
