# Plan — [[14.6.2-converge-storefront-and-standalone-pay]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** make `/store/[slug]` and `/store/[slug]/pay` speak the checkout language converged by [[14.6.1-converge-public-checkout]] — same merchant header/rail (logo or `Monogram` + display name + trust line), same `EmptyState` unavailable/empty/error vocabulary, domain badge tones, public footer with `LanguageSwitcher` — with the standalone page consuming 14.6.1's payment-phase controller and outcome views instead of its local fork, and the public `storefront-*`/`receipt-rail*` BEM plus the `.checkout-*` rules 14.6.1 left alive for this route retired from `globals.css` in favour of the projected utilities.
- **Resultado observável:** a buyer walking storefront → standalone pay → `/pay/[identifier]` sees one identity, one status vocabulary and one furniture set in both locales; `globals.css` keeps no public storefront BEM block, while cart, money, issuance and sessionless behaviour stay identical.

## Estratégia

Presentation only, with every public contract frozen: sessionless routes and rate limits, the browser-local cart (key/envelope, 1–9,999 quantities, recovery notice, exact BigInt per-currency totals never summed), the product-only cart-checkout body with its `201` clear-and-redirect, one-time link issuance, the standalone submit body `{ idempotencyKey, amount, customer }` with policy-exact fields, capability polling and the single opaque unavailable outcome. A convergence that would require touching any of them stops and is reported, never adapted.

The task consumes; it does not invent. 14.6.1 (plan read 2026-09-08) delivers under `src/app/pay/[identifier]/**` the shared 560px branded shell with merchant header and public footer, the privacy `Modal`, the presentational payment/outcome module (eight views over `ProviderStateBadge`, `QrDisplay` by payload, compact `CopyField`) and the `checkout-experience` controller; it also pins `createPollingController`'s path/name/signature for this route and drops expiry countdown, single-use badge and order reference by DTO contract — so this task must not reintroduce them. Consumption is graded: the outcome and payment views and the shell/footer are consumed as they are, and the controller is consumed when its submit/poll seam accepts the standalone endpoints; if it is welded to the link endpoints, the standalone controller stays local but obeys the same phase rule (the form yields, editing never discards an issued payment) and drives 14.6.1's views — a second set of outcome views, rails or tone maps is out of contract, as is any new shared component or `inventory.json` edit.

Retirement is scoped by consumer, not by prefix. Two families lose their last referrer here: the public `.storefront-*` set with `.receipt-rail*`, and the seven `.checkout-*` rules (`card`, `form`, `payment`, `qr`, `copy`, `privacy`, `checkout-v2__total`) 14.6.1 deliberately kept alive because `/store/[slug]/pay` still used them. Both leave `globals.css` only after a grep proves no referrer remains; `.storefront-workspace*`, `.storefront-logo-block|current|actions|staged` and `.storefront-preview*` serve merchant settings and stay for 14.5.3/14.7.1. Replacements use the projected utilities only — `pnpm lint` runs `check-design-tokens`, which rejects raw hex/px/rem/ch and inline styles outside its path-bound allowlist; each page either keeps its own allowlisted accent declaration verbatim or passes the accent to 14.6.1's shell, and neither front edits that script (a needed entry returns to the agente principal). Layouts `boxed|table` survive as utility compositions and both locales change together.

## Áreas afetadas

- `src/app/store/[slug]/**` (routes, loading/error boundaries, colocated tests for assertion retirement only); `src/app/globals.css` and `src/i18n/dictionaries/storefront/{en,pt-BR}.ts` (BEM retirement and the copy delta, shared furniture reusing checkout keys); `DESIGN.md` and [[pop/specs/storefront-and-customization|storefront spec]] (the converged public surface lines).

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** as views/controller de 14.6.1 chegam soldadas aos DTOs de link — mitigação: consumir as views é obrigatório e a fase pode ficar local com desvio registrado; view de desfecho duplicada é `BLOCKED`.
- **Risco:** remover regra ainda usada por outra superfície (`storefront-*` das configurações do merchant, `.checkout-*` de `/pay/**`) — mitigação: grep de referrer antes de cada remoção; `workspace`/`logo-*`/`preview*` permanecem para 14.5.3/14.7.1.
- **Risco:** utility conversion reintroduces raw visual values, an inline style or copy in one locale only — mitigação: `check-design-tokens` dentro do `pnpm lint` e tipos dos dicionários no `pnpm typecheck`.
- **Abortar se:** a convergência exigir mudar body, conjunto de status, rate limit, redirect ou redação de rota pública — sinalizar `blocked: true` com path e linha do contrato afetado.
