# 14.6.1-converge-public-checkout — Converge public checkout


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.6]]
- **Plano:** [[14.6.1-converge-public-checkout.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.6.1-converge-public-checkout.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.6.1-converge-public-checkout.verify]]

## O quê

`/pay/[identifier]` (V1 and V2) reproduces the template checkout: merchant header with Monogram and trust line and merchant theme for V1 too, 560px single-column composition, order summary with single-use badge and expiry countdown, policy-specific forms with e-mail/CPF/CEP format validation and masks, `Pay {total}` submit, the form yielding to the payment phase (editing never wipes an issued QR), QR generated client-side with identity centre-cut on a white frame, compact copy field, immediate polling-failure retry, eight distinct outcome views (created/pending/indeterminate/confirmed/rejected/cancelled/expired/refunded) with success illustration and order reference, privacy notice with modal, footer with powered-by, privacy link and language switcher.

## Por quê

The public checkout audit scores V1 4/10 and V2 5/10: outcome states collapse to one badge, the form never yields to the payment phase, V1 is unbranded and validation is required-only — the buyer-facing surface is the furthest from the template.
