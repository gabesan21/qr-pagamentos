# Plano — [[14.6.1-converge-public-checkout]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** make `/pay/[identifier]` reproduce the template checkout for both eras — merchant header with identity, name and trust line plus merchant theme for V1 too, one 560px column, a policy form with format validation and masks and the total on the submit, a payment phase the form yields to (an issued QR is never wiped by typing), the client-generated QR with the identity centre-cut, a compact copy chip, retry on the first polling failure, eight distinct outcome views, the privacy modal and the public footer with the language switcher — without moving a single public security boundary.
- **Resultado observável:** a buyer opens a V1 or a V2 link, sees the merchant's identity and theme, is told exactly which field is malformed and why, pays a button that names the amount, watches the form give way to the QR, edits nothing away by accident, retries a failed status read at once, and lands on an outcome view that says what happened and how to start over.

## Estratégia

Presentation, one additive read member and one shared client controller. No migration, no new route, no change to the checkout/status request or response bodies, no rate-limit or request-log inventory edit, no service semantics touched. The single new public exposure is the V1 presentation DTO gaining the same `branding` member (localized display name, logo media identifier, resolved theme, validated accent) that 9.3.1 already sanctioned for V2, resolved from the owner record independent of `storefront_enabled` — an already-public disclosure class, never a new one.

Four decisions are pinned so no executor re-decides them. **(1) The pinned DTO boundary is the ceiling, not a suggestion.** The spec fixes the status body at `{ payment: { state, pixCopyPaste?, pixQrCodeUrl? } }` with no identifier, and fixes the V2 checkout DTO as never carrying link state, type or timestamps. So the template's **expiry countdown**, **single-use badge** and **order reference** are not implementable here: they would each open a new public exposure class. They are dropped, not faked, and F04 records why (C09, C12). **(2) One controller, two adapters.** V1 and V2 forms are near-duplicate clients that this task changes identically, so the state machine, validation and polling move into one `checkout-experience` controller consumed by both; `createPollingController` keeps its current module path, name and signature, because `/store/[slug]/pay` imports it and stays untouched. **(3) The asset inventory is closed.** The template's `checkout-success.svg` does not exist here and `brand:check` fails on unknown files, so the success view uses the sanctioned non-color marker composition (badge plus icon plus heading), never a new asset. **(4) BEM retires only where it lost its last referrer.** `/store/[slug]/pay` still consumes `.checkout-card`, `.checkout-form`, `.checkout-payment`, `.checkout-qr`, `.checkout-copy`, `.checkout-privacy` and `.checkout-v2__total`; those survive for 14.6.2. Everything else under `.checkout-*` dies with this diff.

- `src/checkout/public-checkout-presentation.ts` — the additive V1 branding member; `src/i18n/dictionaries/checkout/*`, `src/app/globals.css` and `scripts/check-design-tokens.mjs` — copy in both locales, the retired rules and the pinned accent whitelist; `DESIGN.md`, `pop/specs/checkout-and-order-lifecycle.md` and `src/checkout/AGENTS.md` — the durable record.

## Ordem e paralelismo

1. **Onda 1:** F01 — shell, DTO, every dictionary key and the CSS/whitelist surface the other fronts consume read-only.
2. **Onda 2:** F02 sobre as chaves liberadas. **Onda 3:** F03, que consome as views de F02 e o aviso de privacidade de F01. Não há paralelismo real: há dependência lógica encadeada e um write set por frente.
3. **Onda 4:** F04 sobre o diff integrado. **Integração:** o agente principal valida ownership com `pop/scripts/pop_check_scope.py`, confere que `/store/**`, `src/app/api/**` e os testes estão intactos e roda os critérios `agent`.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** mover `createPollingController` quebra `/store/[slug]/pay`, fora do escopo desta task — mitigação: C07 congela path, nome e assinatura do export. **Risco:** deletar regra `.checkout-*` ainda usada pelo standalone pay — mitigação: C10 exige grep de referência antes de cada remoção. **Risco:** o whitelist de `check-design-tokens.mjs` é por path e exige exatamente um match — mitigação: a declaração de accent vive em um único arquivo novo, com uma entrada só.
- **Abortar se:** a identidade do merchant para V1 exigir leitura fora da linha do link travado, ou qualquer afordância do card exigir campo novo em rota pública — sinalizar `blocked: true` nomeando o campo e o arquivo consultado.
