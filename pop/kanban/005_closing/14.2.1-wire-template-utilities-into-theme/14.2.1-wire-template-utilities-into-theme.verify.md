The self-reference hazard the plan flagged is real in the output and harmless: Tailwind emits `--radius-pill: var(--radius-pill)` and `--font-display: var(--font-display)` inside `@layer theme { :root, :host }` (compile lines 8-9) while the generated literals `999px` and `Sora, sans-serif` sit in an unlayered `:root` (`src/app/globals.css:32,52`). Unlayered declarations outrank layered ones regardless of order, so no cycle forms and `rounded-pill`/`font-display` resolve to the intended values — verified in the compiled CSS, not only by reasoning.
