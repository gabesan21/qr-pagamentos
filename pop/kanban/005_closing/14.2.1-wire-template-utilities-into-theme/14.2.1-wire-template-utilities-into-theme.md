# 14.2.1-wire-template-utilities-into-theme — Wire template utilities into @theme


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.2]]
- **Plano:** [[14.2.1-wire-template-utilities-into-theme.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.2.1-wire-template-utilities-into-theme.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.2.1-wire-template-utilities-into-theme.verify]]

## O quê

The template's utility vocabulary exists as real Tailwind 4 utilities generated from the DTCG tokens: `bg-surface`/`bg-surface-2`, `text-text`/`text-text-2`/`text-text-3`, strong `bg-accent`/`text-accent` with `accent-fg`/`accent-soft`, `success|warning|danger|info` plus their `-soft` companions, `rounded-card`/`rounded-pill`, `shadow-card`, `font-display`/`font-money`, `max-w-app`/`max-w-checkout`; existing shadcn names keep working, the consumers that relied on `accent` meaning the soft tint are migrated, and the token/lint checks stay green.

## Por quê

Today those classes are dead (only `not-found.tsx` uses them) because `@theme inline` exposes shadcn names only, so every page was rebuilt with route-scoped BEM CSS; without the vocabulary no family can be converged onto the template.
