# M-16.1 execution brief

## Objective

Ship the approved standalone PIX flow without changing APIs, checkout service,
provider orchestration, ownership, quote guards, polling, or persistence.

## Strategy

1. Keep amount entry and outcome in the standalone-only storefront mount.
2. Make the legacy pay URL a click-required, read-only continuation.
3. Treat BRL presentation as exact string transformation, never numeric math.
4. Freeze the first client attempt synchronously; reuse it only for duplicate
   mounted invocation and never retry or re-key uncertain work.
5. Make long PIX copy text shrink visually without changing copied payload or QR.

## Boundaries

- Own `src/app/store/[slug]/**`, the shared compact CopyField/payment containment,
  related dictionaries and focused tests.
- Do not edit `src/checkout/standalone-checkout.ts`, provider routes, schemas,
  auth/security code, polling controller, Docker/install files, or dependencies.
- No test, build, Docker, install, commit, or push command is permitted in 004.

## Acceptance criteria

- `NONE` deliberately submits once from the first amount step, with no collection
  notices or footer collection statement, while privacy action remains.
- Other policies collect exactly their required fields after an immutable amount
  summary and expose an edit-amount return before deliberate submission.
- A valid legacy `?amount=` entry is inert until a buyer click; no GET/render/effect
  dispatch occurs. Reload is idle and does not auto-post.
- BRL preserves six-place precision with `R$`, grouping, and comma decimals;
  canonical requests and non-BRL behavior stay unchanged.
- Rapid duplicate submits use one frozen key/payload and produce no automatic retry
  or replacement key.
- A long PIX payload keeps QR and compact copy control contained; copy receives the
  full payload.
- Focused tests are written for the above and reserved for later verification.
