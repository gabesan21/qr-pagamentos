# Plano — [[15.1.2-remove-v1-persistence-and-rebase-migrations]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** remove `payment_link`, `payment_link_order`, `checkout_attempt`, `payment_link_single_use_settlement` and `provider_order.payment_link_order_id` from the declared model, from the migration history and from every static check that pins them, so the database contract describes Commerce V2 only.
- **Resultado observável:** a fresh install applies a V2-only history and creates no V1 table; `prisma/schema.prisma` and `prisma/migrations/**` contain no V1 object; `pnpm db:migration-policy`, `pnpm db:contract-check` and `pnpm db:test` pass against the re-pinned baseline.

## Estratégia

The technical form is a **surgical baseline rebase**, not a drop migration and not a squash. Grep confirms only four directories reference V1 objects: `20260721020000_payment_links`, `20260721040000_payment_link_orders` and `20260721050000_public_checkout_attempts` are wholly V1 and are deleted; `20260721030000_owner_isolation_checkout_policy` loses its `payment_link` statements and keeps the product/user owner-isolation ones. The baseline drops from 19 to 16 directories, `20260721060000_storefront_settings` stays the last baseline id, and the 16 post-baseline migrations stay byte-identical — the "future sorts after the baseline" invariant never moves.

The epoch's fork — extending the closed manifest language with a drop operation — is **not taken**, because its condition never fires: a rebase is a rewrite of reviewed baseline SQL, not something a forward migration must express. Taking it would trade a one-off authorized rewrite for a permanently expressible destructive op whose only gate would be a self-declared manifest field, and it would leave a fresh install creating the four tables just to drop them. So the forward prohibition in [`prisma/AGENTS.md`](../../../../prisma/AGENTS.md) ("never represent … destructive DDL/DML … in a future migration") and the M-2 rule survive **untouched**; what is rewritten is the baseline-immutability line (new pinned set) plus the record that the 2026-09-22 authorization is the single historical exception.

Re-pinning is indivisible: `migration-policy-baseline.json`, the verifier's `BASELINE_INVENTORY_SHA256` anchor and its `19` count, and the fixture count in `migration-policy-contract.mjs` move in one front, because any partial state makes the verifier fail closed. Only then do the dependent artifacts follow: the static contract check (which today asserts the four V1 models and the three V1 migration files), the `db-test` fixtures, the DOX/README sentences and the stale verifier stub in `install/test.sh`. No new DDL, no `db push`, no renaming or renumbering of surviving directories, and no trigger to replace the V1 active-dependency trigger — V2 dependency enforcement is service-fenced by contract. Specs stay with [[15.1.3-rewrite-contracts-and-parity-without-v1]].

## Áreas afetadas

- `prisma/schema.prisma` — the four V1 models, their relation fields on `User`/`Product`/`CatalogCurrencyPair`/`ProviderOrder` and the `payment_link_order_id` column with its two unique maps — and `prisma/migrations/**`, where three baseline directories are deleted, one is edited and everything after the baseline stays untouched.
- `prisma/migration-policy-baseline.json` · `pop/scripts/migration-policy.mjs` · `pop/scripts/migration-policy-contract.mjs` — re-pinned inventory, new anchor digest and count; `pop/scripts/db-contract-check.mjs` and `db-test.mjs` lose the V1 assertions and the V1 `payment_link` fixture block.
- `prisma/bootstrap.sql` · `prisma/AGENTS.md` · `README.md` (Database foundation) · `install/test.sh` — V1 grant residue, the pinned-baseline sentences and the stubbed verifier line.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** the SHA-pinned verifier fails closed on a half-applied rebase (inventory re-pinned, anchor stale, or the reverse) — mitigação: F01 owns inventory, anchor and fixture count together and recomputes the anchor last, from the final bytes.
- **Risco:** every database created before the rebase becomes unmigratable (`_prisma_migrations` still holds the three deleted ids) and pre-rebase backups are no longer restorable — mitigação: authorized by the 2026-09-22 destructive decision; the local environment is destroyed and recreated, and C10/C11 carry it to the human checklist. No compatibility path is invented.
- **Risco:** the V1 `payment_link_require_active_dependencies` trigger was the database-boundary proof of product archival; deleting it removes that fixture — mitigação: F02 records the coverage delta in its Result (V2 enforcement is service-fenced) and adds no new trigger.
- **Risco:** the ignored `src/generated/prisma/` client and past memory ledgers still name V1 — mitigação: the client is regenerated by `pnpm db:generate` (C08) and never hand-edited; memory is append-only history and is never rewritten, this task's own ledger records the rebase.
- **Abortar se:** [[15.1.1-remove-v1-application-surfaces]] has no ledger in `pop/memory/`, or `src/`/`tests/` still reference a V1 model — sinalizar `blocked: true` com `arquivo:linha`.
- **Abortar se:** limpar `20260721030000_owner_isolation_checkout_policy` exigir mexer em statement de product/user/owner-isolation, ou se alguma migração, tabela ou constraint V2 depender de objeto V1 — sinalizar `blocked: true` com a evidência; nunca criar DDL nova para compensar.
