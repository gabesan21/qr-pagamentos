# M-18.1-payment-link-create-ux — Payment link create/edit UX

- **Origem:** [[pop/MODIFICATIONS|M-18]]
- **Plano:** [[M-18.1-payment-link-create-ux.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[M-18.1-payment-link-create-ux.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[M-18.1-payment-link-create-ux.verify]]

## O quê

Bring the merchant V2 payment-link create/edit experience in line with the rest of the panel: a BRL live cash mask on the fixed-amount field when the selected pair is BRL (mirroring H-16.1, exact decimal up to six places, with the visual field unnamed and the canonical value posted through a hidden input that stays omitted until dirty on edit), an honest additive ISO `currencyCode` projection on the currency-pair catalog so the mask and the preview never guess a currency, an expiry control shown in the browser's local clock that converts to UTC on submit (untouched edit stays absent, clearing posts empty, no hydration mismatch), and copy/layout harmonization of the composition, method, and type cards, the heading/divider and the CTA with the design system across `pt-BR`/`en` without breaking accessibility or mobile.

## Por quê

The create/edit flow is the merchant's main entry point for new payment links; its amount, expiry, and copy still diverge from the panel's established money-input and design-system conventions already applied elsewhere, and the user approved completing that convergence now.
