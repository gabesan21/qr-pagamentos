# 14.1.1-repair-product-lifecycle-actions — Repair product lifecycle actions


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.1]]
- **Plano:** [[14.1.1-repair-product-lifecycle-actions.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.1.1-repair-product-lifecycle-actions.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.1.1-repair-product-lifecycle-actions.verify]]

## O quê

Archive and activate/deactivate on `/catalog/products/[id]` actually submit after confirmation; archived products stay inspectable in a read-only form; product and category create/edit failures return to the originating form with the typed values and a visible error instead of redirecting to the list.

## Por quê

The two primary product lifecycle actions are unreachable from the UI (confirm dialogs call `requestSubmit()` on refs never attached) and every server-side failure discards the merchant's input — the system is not functional for catalog management.
