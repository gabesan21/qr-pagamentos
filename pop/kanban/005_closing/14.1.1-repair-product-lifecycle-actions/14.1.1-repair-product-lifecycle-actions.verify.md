### Qualidade da implementação

| Severidade | Achado | Evidência | Correção necessária |

| sugestão | The form-supplied `id` is interpolated unencoded into the `Location` path; a malformed id (CR/LF) would make `NextResponse` throw inside the very `catch`, turning a handled failure into a 500. Not an open redirect (the `/catalog/products/` prefix cannot leave the origin) and it needs an authenticated owner, but the plan promised nothing from the client names a path | `src/app/products/route.ts:27-31` | `encodeURIComponent(id)` when building the target |
| sugestão | Under `categories=failed` raised by *any* category mutation, an unrelated row holding an old draft reopens its edit form with stale values — the "clear on a load without notice" mitigation does not cover this path | `src/app/(merchant)/catalog/categories/category-row-actions.tsx:42-57` | when saving one draft, evict the other `qr-catalog-draft:` entries (one draft at a time) |
| nit | The detail page shows two identical "Save" controls (form-owned and page-level) — the same duplication C10 removed on `/products/new`, but **pre-existing** and outside the card's request | `product-detail-client.tsx:193` + `product-form.tsx:380` | follow-up for Epoch 14, not this task |
