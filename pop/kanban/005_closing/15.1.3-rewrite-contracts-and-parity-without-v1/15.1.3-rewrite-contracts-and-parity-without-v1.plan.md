# Plano — [[15.1.3-rewrite-contracts-and-parity-without-v1]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** the durable contracts (three specs, two DOX files) and the frontend parity records describe Commerce V2 only — no V1 statement, table name, module name, route or obligation record survives 15.1.1's deletion.
- **Resultado observável:** a planner or judge reading `pop/specs/checkout-and-order-lifecycle.md`, its two neighbours and the DOX tree finds no surface that no longer exists, and the parity gate again binds a V2-only current-route inventory.

## Estratégia

15.1.1 deletes the code; this task makes the written contracts match it. The rewrite is **subtractive by default**: V1-scoped statements are deleted and V1-era labels renamed, while no V2 sentence is reworded, reordered or "improved". The sections Commerce V2 orders (8.1.x), persistence shape/fences, standalone 9.2.1, buyer experience 9.2.2, public checkout 9.3.1, terminal states 9.3.2, issuance 9.1.3 and analytics 8.4.1 become the normative body exactly as written today, minus their V1 fences and cross-references. Names carrying a `V1` suffix that survive as V2 contracts (settlement map, customer snapshot, Brazil address) lose the suffix in prose; the code identifier `CustomerSnapshotV1` stays — it is a live type, not an era.

The header is rebuilt, not appended to: today's `Updated` line repeats the same 8.x/9.x changelog four times. One lean `Updated` line naming this task replaces it; the history is memory's job (`005_closing` ledger/entries), never the spec's. The V1 settle-wiring open question is already `answered`: the contract drops its "Open" bullet and the 9.2.1 sentence that deferred it, and nothing in `pop/specs/` links it any more.

Parity is the risky half and gets its own front. The records are **generated**: the canonical `--refresh-semantic-contract` regenerates the three semantic families and re-binds `source.path`/`sha256`, but `assertRefreshInvariants` demands an identical record count and ID set, and `refreshCurrentRouteSources` aborts with `PARITY_CURRENT_ROUTE_REFRESH_MISSING_INVENTORY` for every `current-route` record whose file is gone — and `/orders/[id]` and `/admin/orders/[id]` have exactly such records. The official command alone therefore cannot express retirement, and hand-editing is forbidden by the README. F02 resolves it the way the epoch already authorizes for 15.1.2's migration language: extend the tool with **one closed, fail-closed retirement operation** (a record is retired only when its bound current file is absent from the repository, never by name or flag), then run the canonical refresh and the check. Semantic records self-heal — their IDs derive from the template source and their targets are recomputed from the live inventory.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** deleting a V2 contract along with its V1 fence — mitigation: subtractive-only edit and C3's section/bullet preservation check before the front reports `concluída`.
- **Risco:** the refresh aborts (`*_MISSING_INVENTORY`, `*_AMBIGUOUS`, `PARITY_REFRESH_INVARIANT_*`) — mitigation: retire first through the closed operation, then refresh; never hand-edit a record.
- **Risco:** a semantic record loses every concrete target (`*_EVIDENCE_IDENTITY_INVALID`) — mitigation: F02 reports the record ids as `BLOCKED` instead of inventing a target; root `AGENTS.md` ceiling (75 lines, ~61 net of the DOX block) — mitigation: C6 forbids growth there.
- **Abortar se:** the retirement operation cannot be expressed without weakening a fail-closed guarantee of the checker, or 15.1.1 is not integrated → `blocked: true` with the diagnostic/record id as evidence.
