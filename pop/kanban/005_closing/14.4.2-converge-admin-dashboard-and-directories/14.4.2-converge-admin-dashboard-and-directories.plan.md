# Plano — [[14.4.2-converge-admin-dashboard-and-directories]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** bring `/admin`, `/admin/orders**` and `/admin/payment-links**` to the template's operations model — segmented period control, real 5/4/3 grid, delta chip, per-card skeletons, inline error+retry, the filters and columns the template designs, live drill-downs between dashboard, orders and links, domain badges everywhere — without weakening the read-only administrator boundary.
- **Resultado observável:** an administrator switches the dashboard period without a full navigation, filters orders by provider state and merchant, jumps from a top merchant to that merchant's orders, opens a link from an order and its orders from a link, copies a link's public URL, and meets no BEM composition or raw enum chip on these pages.

## Estratégia

Presentation and query shape only. These surfaces stay read-only: no form, action or mutation is added, every new read re-authorizes the administrator through the delivered `ADMIN_GLOBAL` services, and no field beyond the delivered projections plus the `username`/`deletedAt` attribution leaves persistence. A template control the data cannot honour is recorded as a gap in the spec, never faked. Four decisions are pinned here so no executor re-decides them, each restated in the slice that implements it. **(1) Orders filters** gain `state` (the eight provider states plus one explicit stateless option mapped to `state: null`) and `merchant` (text, exact case-insensitive owner-username equality) on top of `source`/`money`/`from`/`to`/`link` — 7 registered filters, inside the spec's cap of eight — and `source` regains its full `LINK`/`STANDALONE`/`AD_HOC` vocabulary. **(2) Link filters** gain the same `merchant` plus `money` (`USD`/`FIAT` through the active exchange-currency pointer on the link's currency pair); the template's pair select is not reproducible literally because registered filters are closed static enums while pair labels are dynamic registry rows. **(3) No `outcome` filter:** the current local outcome is the newest append in an append-only ledger, and "latest is X" is not expressible in the Prisma adapter without raw SQL or a denormalized column — the column keeps rendering, the filter is a recorded gap. **(4) No unified V1+V2 table:** V1 orders live in another table, carry no owner attribution and are served by a byte-frozen service, so one keyset page cannot span both — the V1 ledger keeps its own section, restyled into the template's table look inside the admin route's own file.

The dashboard period control becomes client URL state composed from the owned `Tabs`/`TabsList` pill variant, committing with `router.replace` inside a transition like the directory toolbar; the 5/4/3 split moves to `lg:col-span-*` and the whole `.admin-dashboard*` block leaves `globals.css`; the delta chip is `StatCard`'s `trend`, fed only by counts the projection already returns. No sparkline — neither the projection nor the template's own `DashboardStats` carries a series. Badges adopt `ProviderStateBadge`, `LocalOutcomeBadge` and `LinkLifecycleBadge` with caller-supplied localized labels; a null provider state and `LOCAL_CANCELLED` have no member in those unions and render through `StatusBadge` with the matching tone. Pagination keeps previous/next only: page numbers and totals are a standing data-directory fork, not a defect to repair here.

## Ordem e paralelismo

1. **Ondas:** F01 e F03 em paralelo (write sets e lógica disjuntos) → F02 (após o contrato de F03) → F04 sobre o diff integrado.
2. **Integração:** o agente principal valida ownership com `pop_check_scope.py`, confere que o href de F01 usa o `filter.merchant` registrado por F02, e roda os critérios `agent`.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** uma extensão de filtro alarga o que sai da persistência, ou uma edição em view compartilhada muda a superfície do merchant — mitigação: filtros só restringem colunas existentes, DTOs de linha ficam idênticos, e as views compartilhadas recebem prop opcional ou só a composição do estado indisponível (C10). Suítes admin fixadas na BEM e no select de duas fontes quebram na integração: não são tocadas aqui, e C12 as registra na checklist de 14.4.4.
- **Abortar se:** um controle do template exigir nova API, migração ou mutação numa superfície read-only — sinalizar `blocked: true` com o controle, o dado ausente e o arquivo consultado.
