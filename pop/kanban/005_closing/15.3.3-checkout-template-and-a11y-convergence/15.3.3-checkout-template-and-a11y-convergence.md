# 15.3.3-checkout-template-and-a11y-convergence — Checkout template and a11y convergence

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.3]]
- **Plano:** [[15.3.3-checkout-template-and-a11y-convergence.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.3.3-checkout-template-and-a11y-convergence.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.3.3-checkout-template-and-a11y-convergence.verify]]

## O quê

Checkout surfaces gain an `aria-live="polite"` region around the state badge, the INDETERMINATE line in `text-warning-on-soft` over `bg-warning-soft`, a `loading.tsx` that reads the `qr_locale` cookie, the declared `max-w-checkout`/`font-display`/`bg-accent` utilities in place of ad-hoc forms, a 44 px privacy button and a reduced-motion-gated scroll to the first invalid field.

## Por quê

Findings A9–A11 on the buyer surface: the declared template vocabulary and AA rules exist but the checkout bypasses them.
