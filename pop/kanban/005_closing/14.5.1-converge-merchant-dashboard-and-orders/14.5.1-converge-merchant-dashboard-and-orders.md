# 14.5.1-converge-merchant-dashboard-and-orders — Converge merchant dashboard and orders


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.5]]
- **Plano:** [[14.5.1-converge-merchant-dashboard-and-orders.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.5.1-converge-merchant-dashboard-and-orders.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.5.1-converge-merchant-dashboard-and-orders.verify]]

## O quê

`/`, `/orders`, `/orders/[id]` and `/orders/v2/[id]` reproduce the template: dashboard with segmented period control, by-state/by-source chart tabs, four inventory cards, clickable recent orders and leading products with ids, storefront-disabled banner, first-run empty state and error/stale/retry strip; orders directory with provider-state and local-outcome filters and a currency-pair filter where the projection allows; order detail with the template outcome editor (select + note + confirm dialog, gated on `confirmed`), comment composer with Monogram and newest-first timeline, payment-data card where available, link card with lifecycle badge and link to the link detail; domain badges adopted, unavailable states as illustrations.

## Por quê

The merchant commerce audit rates dashboard and orders medium-low to low: chart content differs, rows are dead, filters are missing and the outcome/comment interactions are structurally different from the template.
