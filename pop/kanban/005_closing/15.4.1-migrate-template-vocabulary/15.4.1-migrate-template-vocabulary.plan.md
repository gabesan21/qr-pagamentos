# Plano — [[15.4.1-migrate-template-vocabulary]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** replace the legacy shadcn vocabulary with the declared template utilities in the screen families the template maps, keep the unmapped families on a recorded exception, and clear the `admin:source-check` raw-control/local-variant findings (F6) by composing owned primitives and by repairing the gate's own classification where the contract already sanctions the construct.
- **Resultado observável:** the migrated families read one vocabulary (`text-text-2`, `bg-surface`, `rounded-card`, `font-display`, `max-w-app/checkout/auth-form`), rendering is unchanged because every substitution is value-identical, and `pnpm admin:source-check` reports `raw_controls=0 local_variants=0 inline_styles=0` at the phase gate.

## Estratégia

The migration is a **closed, value-identical rename**, never a redesign: each pair in the map resolves to the same variable in all six themes (`src/app/globals.css:106-136,1308-1336`), so contrast, geometry and captures do not move. Anything outside the map stays untouched — the safe way to end a two-vocabulary drift is to move only what provably renders the same. Two boundaries shape the write sets. **Route files** (`page.tsx`, `loading.tsx`, `error.tsx`) are hashed as `current-route` obligation sources (`scripts/check-frontend-template-parity-contract.mjs:383,766`), so editing one raises `PARITY_SOURCE_HASH_STALE` whose repair lives in `docs/frontend-template-parity/**` — task 15.4.2's F2/F5; they are excluded here and their residual legacy uses become a follow-up, not a defect. **`src/components/ui/**` and `src/app/design-system/**`** are excluded too: DESIGN.md keeps the shadcn utilities working inside the owned primitives, the `owners` set is frozen, and the specimen is evidence-bound.

F6 is resolved by contract, not by force. Where an owner exists, compose it — `language-switcher.tsx`'s raw `<select>` becomes the owned `NativeSelect`, the shell account-panel submit becomes the owned `Button` in the same shape `src/app-shell/shell-navigation.tsx:224` already uses. Where the finding contradicts a sanctioned construct, the **gate's classification** is the defect: `app-shell__account-panel*` is chrome from the sanctioned `app-shell.css` and is merely missing beside its 20 siblings in the allow list; hidden and `sr-only` inputs are native form bridges DESIGN.md itself sanctions (14.4.3 hidden-input bridge, 14.5.2 radio-card group) and no owner exists to compose; the `--storefront-accent` style at `src/app/admin/accounts/[id]/page.tsx:193` is already allow-listed by `scripts/check-design-tokens.mjs:9-14`, so two gates disagreeing is the bug. Each exemption is typed, path- or attribute-bound and commented with its reason — never a blanket waiver.

## Mapa de substituição (fechado)

| Legado | Declarado | Prova |
|---|---|---|
| `text-muted-foreground` | `text-text-2` | `--muted-foreground` and `--color-text-2` = `--color-text-secondary` |
| `text-card-foreground`, `text-foreground` | `text-text` | both = `--color-text-primary` |
| `bg-card` · `bg-muted` as surface · `bg-background` | `bg-surface` · `bg-surface-2` · `bg-bg` | same `--color-surface-raised/secondary/page` |
| `rounded-lg` | `rounded-card` | `--radius-lg` and `--radius-card` = `--radius-panel` |
| `font-[family-name:var(--font-display)]` | `font-display` | same variable |
| `max-w-[var(--shell-max\|--checkout-max\|--auth-form-max)]` | `max-w-app\|checkout\|auth-form` | `--container-*` bind exactly these |

**Never substituted:** `text-text-3` (tertiary ≠ secondary — a contrast change, not a rename); `bg-primary`/`text-primary-foreground` (in `pix-paper` that pair is `#00b8a0`/`#1e2a26` ≈ 6.3:1 while `bg-accent`/`text-accent-fg` would be `#00b8a0`/`#ffffff` ≈ 2.5:1 — AA wins over the audit palette, so A9's "`bg-primary` in accent role" does not authorize the swap); `max-w-[var(--layout-max)]` (65ch prose measure, no declared utility); `bg-destructive`; radius/shadow other than `rounded-card`/`shadow-card`; any size, spacing, state, copy or dictionary key.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** editar arquivo de rota deixa `frontend-parity:check` stale e invade 15.4.2 — mitigação: `page.tsx`/`loading.tsx`/`error.tsx` ficam fora de todo `owns`; resíduo vira follow-up na memory.
- **Risco:** `check-design-tokens.mjs` autoriza os 5 estilos de acento por path+padrão exatos, e a paridade casa componente por basename — mitigação: essas expressões são `must_not_edit` e criar/renomear/apagar arquivo sob `src/` é proibido.
- **Risco:** teste que afirma classe legada (`src/app/admin/page.test.tsx`) — mitigação: a frente dona do arquivo migrado ajusta só a asserção que nomeia a classe migrada; asserção em arquivo excluído volta como achado, não é editada.
- **Abortar se:** o escopo exigir contrato novo, componente novo, exceção de gate além das três nomeadas ou edição em path excluído — sinalizar `blocked: true` com path e regra violada.
