## O quê

Match link directories, create/edit/detail, lifecycle actions, sharing, versioning, and nested order drill-downs under `src/app/(merchant)/links/**` using the new design system and existing catalog/payment-link contracts.

## Por quê

Payment links are the merchant-facing core of the commerce surface; applying the template here unifies the owner experience while preserving link ownership, versioning, sharing, and order-derivation rules.
