# 15.3.1-checkout-explicit-states-without-retry — Checkout explicit states without retry

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.3]]
- **Plano:** [[15.3.1-checkout-explicit-states-without-retry.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.3.1-checkout-explicit-states-without-retry.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.3.1-checkout-explicit-states-without-retry.verify]]

## O quê

On `/pay/[identifier]` and `/store/[slug]/pay`: a present payload renders QR plus copy-paste; `PENDING` without payload, `INDETERMINATE`, a failed QR generation, a failed status read and a failed submit each render an explicit named state with **Start over** as the only action. The `1s·2^n` backoff, `retryPoll`, `checkoutCheckAgain`, `checkoutPollErrorBanner`, `checkoutRetryStatus`, `checkoutRetry`, `checkoutGeneratingQr` and same-attempt resubmission are removed; the 5 s status read stays.

## Por quê

Decision 3: no retry rule in the checkout; the current UI pulses forever or offers actions that cannot succeed (findings A6, retry surfaces).
