# 12.4.3-remodel-administrator-account-management — Remodel administrator account management

> **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/12-frontend-template-remodel|Phase 12.4]]
> **Plano:** [[12.4.3-remodel-administrator-account-management.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[12.4.3-remodel-administrator-account-management.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[12.4.3-remodel-administrator-account-management.verify]]

## O quê

Match account creation, directory, profile editor, access, TOTP recovery, storefront settings, and deletion states under `src/app/admin/accounts/**` onto the new design system while preserving authorization and privileged-action boundaries.

## Por quê

Phase 12.4 remodels every administrator surface onto the approved template; the accounts subtree is the privileged identity and storefront-administration hub, so it must reuse the established shell, tokens, and read-only/privileged-action contracts from Phase 12.3.
