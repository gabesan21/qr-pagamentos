# 15.2.2-fail-closed-on-documented-creation-errors — Fail closed on documented creation errors

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.2]]
- **Plano:** [[15.2.2-fail-closed-on-documented-creation-errors.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.2.2-fail-closed-on-documented-creation-errors.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.2.2-fail-closed-on-documented-creation-errors.verify]]

## O quê

A `POST /orders/onramp` answer with a documented deterministic `code` (`order.quote_not_found`, `order.quote_expired`, `order.exchange_not_configured`, `order.payment_method_not_available`, `orders.deposit_bank_account_not_found`, `order.deposit_fields_required`, `order.deposit_fields_validation_failed`) marks the attempt and order as failed and answers the existing redacted pre-dispatch outcome; timeouts, 5xx and unparseable 201 stay `INDETERMINATE`; there is never a second POST. Applies to the V2 link checkout and the standalone checkout.

## Por quê

Configuration errors currently read as 'confirming with your bank' forever (finding A4); the buyer needs an explicit outcome and the merchant a diagnosable state.
