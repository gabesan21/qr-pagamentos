# Plan — [[12.2.2-reconcile-template-identity-and-assets]]

- **Stage:** 002_planning · **Owner:** agent planner
## Objective and expected result

- **Objective:** replace the superseded settlement-mark family with the approved template identity and install all supplied application-owned artwork through one deterministic, licensed, safe, hash-bound asset pipeline.
- **Observable result:** inline identity, generated static lockups, illustrations, fallbacks, six theme swatches, and 16/32/48 favicon frames reproduce their approved targets; drift, unsafe SVG, unknown bytes, incomplete provenance, or merchant-media crossover fails closed.

## Strategy
Keep `docs/template/**` immutable and non-runtime. Bind each of its 17 SVGs to its parity obligation, byte length, SHA-256, intrinsic geometry, role, source commit, and exact project-use authorization; do not claim authorship, ownership, or license terms beyond recorded evidence. Canonical production sources live under `src/brand/`; generated public derivatives and their manifest remain generator-owned.
Preserve the four public identity IDs to avoid consumer API churn, but replace their geometry/composition with the template QR glyph, Sora wordmark outlines supplied by 12.2.1, and the supplied merchant/store fallback. Once no production or provenance contract references IBM Plex Sans, remove that transient dependency from package/lock through F04. Emit other artwork under a distinct application-asset namespace; it is never merchant media. Use an element/attribute allowlist, local-only references, deterministic serialization, closed output inventory, and byte/hash/dimension checks.
Visual proof is asset-scoped: a fresh Chromium contact sheet compares source targets with production derivatives at intrinsic and representative UI sizes, both on-color modes and all six swatches. Route migrations and the final design-system specimen stay with later tasks.

## Affected areas

- `src/brand/**`, `scripts/{brand-contract,generate-brand-assets}*` — canonical identity, source register, generation, checks, and DOX.
- `public/brand/**`, `public/application-assets/**`, `src/app/favicon.ico` — generated outputs; font provenance/checker/tests and package/lock form the narrow F04 cleanup boundary.
- `tests/**`, `scripts/*brand-assets-evidence*`, `artifacts/brand-assets/**` — fresh visual/runtime-independent evidence without package-script ownership.

## Gaps and preflight
- **Execution input:** 12.2.1 must provide pinned licensed Sora bytes/outlines and canonical six-theme token values; absence/incompatibility is `BLOCKED`, not permission to use live text, Google Fonts, IBM Plex substitution, or copied palette authority.
- **Observed baseline:** `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs --check` verified 12 current assets; clean-worktree `pnpm` could not open its dependency-store SQLite and no local Vitest existed. Executor must install/link the pinned environment before tests.
- **Return gap:** F01/F02 are functionally intact, but font provenance still requires the obsolete IBM transition and the manifest hash-binds the corrected source register; F04 removes only that contract gap, then F02 replays only manifest generation/check.

## Risks and abort conditions
- Source approval is not an ownership claim; missing or contradictory usage authority blocks only the affected asset lane and must identify the exact record.
- Abort on unavailable licensed Sora derivation, mutation of the three pinned target families, mismatch with the 17 source hashes/parity IDs, unsafe/unparseable SVG, non-deterministic output, or need to edit `src/media/**`, DB/API/security, route consumers, tokens, or template bytes.
