# 14.6.2-converge-storefront-and-standalone-pay — Converge storefront and standalone pay


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.6]]
- **Plano:** [[14.6.2-converge-storefront-and-standalone-pay.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.6.2-converge-storefront-and-standalone-pay.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.6.2-converge-storefront-and-standalone-pay.verify]]

## O quê

`/store/[slug]` and `/store/[slug]/pay` extrapolate the converged checkout language: the same merchant header/rail, badge tones, unavailable and error vocabulary (`EmptyState`), footer with language switcher, `Monogram` and domain badges, the standalone payment reusing the checkout payment-phase and outcome views; the `storefront-*`/`receipt-rail*` BEM blocks are retired.

## Por quê

Both public storefront pages score 4–5/10 and use a hand-rolled BEM system with a header rail distinct from the checkout's; once 14.6.1 fixes the checkout, these pages must share its vocabulary rather than diverge again.
