## O quê

Remove the seven residual Prisma calls to the deleted V1 models that 15.1.4 found failing `tsc` (`src/auth/administration.ts`, `src/auth/payment-link-v2.ts`, `src/integrations/nautt/provider-order-store.ts` and correlated files, plus their test fakes in `administration.test.ts`, `profile-concurrency.test.ts` and the nautt tests), and replace the storefront's V1-derived `products` projection with a V2-only rule, syncing `pop/specs/storefront-and-customization.md` (line 27 today) and `src/integrations/nautt/AGENTS.md` (`paymentLinkOrderId` line). The exact list is in [[15.1.4-phase-verification.verify]] (round 1, C1 and follow-up F1). Nothing else: webhook URL registration untouched, no local webhook test, no migration.

## Por quê

`pnpm check` cannot pass on `develop` while the V2-only schema is called with V1 model names; 15.1.4 is blocked on this exact gap and re-runs C1 once it closes.
