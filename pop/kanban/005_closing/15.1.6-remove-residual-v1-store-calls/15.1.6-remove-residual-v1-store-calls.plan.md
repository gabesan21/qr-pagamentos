# Plano — [[15.1.6-remove-residual-v1-store-calls]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** delete the last Prisma calls that still name the dropped V1 models/column, and give the public storefront `products` projection a V2-only rule — no new table, column or migration.
- **Resultado observável:** every persistence call in the touched files resolves against a model/field that exists in `prisma/schema.prisma`; admin deletion, V2 link creation, provider-order claim and `/store/[slug]` keep their current behavior minus V1.

## Estratégia

The seven call sites listed in C1 of [[15.1.4-phase-verification.verify]] split into two kinds. **Pure removal**, where the logic existed only for V1: the `deactivatePaymentLinks` member of the administration store (V1 soft-delete withdrawal — `deactivatePaymentLinksV2` already covers the live model), the `db.paymentLink.count` half of `identifierTaken`, and the whole `paymentLinkOrderId` surface (`StoredProviderOrder` field, both store implementations, `QuoteOwnershipStore`, the `createOrder` parameter and the `undefined` placeholder its two checkout callers pass). **Replacement**, where the behavior survives under V2: the storefront `products` projection.

**Storefront rule (decided here):** a member of `products` is an active product that appears in the lines of at least one active, unexpired **`REUSABLE`** Commerce V2 link of the same owner, and `paymentLinkIdentifier` is that link's identifier (earliest created, deterministic id tiebreak). *Justification:* it is the literal V2 reading of the spec's "at least one active payment link (each linking to its checkout)" — the one candidate that keeps every listed product payable, while counting any active link would publish every product a `SINGLE_USE` cart-checkout link (9.1.3) or a consumed single-use link happens to name, i.e. change the public surface. The member shape, the redaction boundary and the 9.1.2 catalog/cart path stay exactly as they are.

`paymentLink` is also the live **V2 relation name** (`OrderV2`, `CheckoutAttemptV2`, `PaymentLinkV2Line`): only the `client.paymentLink`/`db.paymentLink` delegates and the `Product.paymentLinks` relation are V1. The financial-edit gate is already V2-only (`checkoutAttemptV2.count`) and is not touched. This task runs no gate: `pnpm check` and the suite belong to [[15.1.4-phase-verification]], which re-runs C1 after this lands.

## Áreas afetadas

- `src/auth/**` — the administration store's V1 deactivation member and the V1 half of the V2 identifier-collision check, with their test doubles.
- `src/integrations/nautt/**` (+ the DOX line that documents the attach) and `src/checkout/**` — the `paymentLinkOrderId` surface across store, port and service, plus the two `createOrder` call sites carrying the removed positional argument and their asserts.
- `src/storefront/**`, `src/app/store/[slug]/page.test.tsx` and `pop/specs/storefront-and-customization.md` — the `products` projection query, record type, fixtures and the boundary line that states the rule.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** a name-based sweep deletes live V2 surfaces (`paymentLink` relation in `order-v2-view.ts`, `order-v2-directory.ts`, `public-checkout-v2.ts`) — mitigação: remove only the delegate calls and the `Product.paymentLinks` relation named in C1; every other `paymentLink` occurrence stays.
- **Risco:** touching the financial-edit gate while removing V1 counting — mitigação: `payment-link-v2.ts` is changed only at `identifierTaken`; the `checkoutAttemptV2` attempt count is already V2-only and out of reach.
- **Risco:** the `products` rule silently changes the public list — mitigação: the rule above is the contract; the spec line is updated in the same task and the projection fixtures assert the new predicate.
- **Risco:** the gitignored `src/generated/prisma` client on this host is stale and can mask or invent type errors — mitigação: correctness is judged against `prisma/schema.prisma`; generated code is never read as truth, edited or committed.
- **Abortar se:** any call can only be preserved by adding a column, table or migration, or by editing a spec other than the storefront one — sinalizar `blocked: true` com o call site e o contrato em conflito.
