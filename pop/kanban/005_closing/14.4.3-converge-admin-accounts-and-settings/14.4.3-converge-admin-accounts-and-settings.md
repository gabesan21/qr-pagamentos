# 14.4.3-converge-admin-accounts-and-settings — Converge admin accounts and settings


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.4]]
- **Plano:** [[14.4.3-converge-admin-accounts-and-settings.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.4.3-converge-admin-accounts-and-settings.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.4.3-converge-admin-accounts-and-settings.verify]]

## O quê

`/admin/accounts`, `/admin/accounts/[id]` and `/admin/settings` reproduce the template: create-account modal with initial status, password generator, strength meter and copy field; tabbed account editor (identity/access/storefront/preferences/danger) with segmented role/status/language controls, checkout-policy card grid, theme swatch grid and layout segmented control, deleted-account facts-only view with banner; settings with scroll-spy nav, functional global payment settings switches, exchange-currency UUID columns and inactive rows, per-section success banners, immediate language apply.

## Por quê

The admin family audit rates these pages medium-low: inline create form, stacked native-select editor, inert global payment settings and hidden UUID facts diverge from the template's designed administration surface.
