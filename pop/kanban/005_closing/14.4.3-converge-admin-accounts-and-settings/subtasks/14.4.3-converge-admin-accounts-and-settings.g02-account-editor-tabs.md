## Contrato de execução

- Tabs replace the anchored section cards: one client tab controller over the owned `SimpleTabs`, each panel keeping its own native POST form with the delivered action and field names. The active tab persists in the URL (hash or a `tab` param the page reads directly) and is restored on load; never route the tab through the directory query grammar.
- Role, status and locale become segmented controls submitting the same `role`/`status`/`locale` values; a destructive value (demote to `USER`, `DISABLED`) opens the owned `ConfirmDialog` before the form submits. The frozen routes keep landing on `/admin`, which already toasts — do not re-point them and do not fake an on-page result.
- Checkout policy renders `CHECKOUT_DATA_POLICIES` as a selectable card grid and storefront theme as the swatch grid (same asset path the settings appearance section uses), layout as a segmented control. Display names use `LocalizedFieldGroup`; because its inputs are controlled and unnamed, mirror the two values into hidden inputs named exactly `storefrontDisplayNamePtBr` and `storefrontDisplayNameEn`. All nine storefront fields keep their delivered names, limits and help text.
- Editor and reset outcomes (`?editor=`, `?reset=`) raise one toast through `NoticeToast` with the same `Alert` inside `<noscript>`; the password reset keeps the delivered email-link flow — presented template-style (confirm then result), never as a temp-password modal, since the route returns no password.
- A deleted target renders the deleted banner plus the facts card only: no tabs, no danger panel, no mutation form.
- Retire `AccountMutationForm` with its unit test in the same change and drop the now-orphan `.admin-confirm-actions` rule. Leave `.admin-account*` alone: its consumer is `src/app/orders/order-views.tsx` (V1 order list), owned by another task.
- Every new string lands in `src/i18n/dictionaries/admin-user-profile/{en,pt-BR}.ts` with both locales in the same commit.
