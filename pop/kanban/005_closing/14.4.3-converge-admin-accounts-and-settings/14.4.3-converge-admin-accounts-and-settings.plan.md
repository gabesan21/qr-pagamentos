# Plano — [[14.4.3-converge-admin-accounts-and-settings]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** bring `/admin/accounts`, `/admin/accounts/[id]` and `/admin/settings` to the template's administration surface — create modal, tabbed editor with segmented/grid controls, scroll-spy settings with operable global payments — composed over the delivered mutations.
- **Resultado observável:** an administrator creates an account in a modal with a generated, measured and copyable password; edits one account through five tabs whose active tab survives a reload; and works `/admin/settings` with a scroll-spy nav, operable BRL/PIX switches and feedback inside the section that saved.

## Estratégia

Presentation and interaction only, over the delivered native POST routes. `/admin/users/[id]/{role,status,password,delete}` stay byte-frozen: the UI wraps them, never re-points them — so their `/admin` landing keeps toasting there, and per-save toasts on the editor cover only the routes that return to it (`?editor=`, `?reset=`). One exception is decided here: `POST /admin/users` (create, not frozen) re-points from `/admin?success=created` to `/admin/accounts?success=created|error=create-failed`, because `resolveAdminAccountsDirectoryQuery` already parses that closed notice pair, nothing produces it today, and the 14.3.2 rule in `DESIGN.md` requires a mutation to return to the page that submitted it.

Three template obligations are unreachable under that boundary and are delivered as recorded gaps, never as silent omissions or as service changes: (a) **initial status** — `POST /admin/users` and `createUser` write `status: "ACTIVE"` and accept no status field, so no dead control is rendered; (b) **exchange-currency UUID columns and inactive rows** — `listMappings` is active-only and redacted to `{code,label}` by decision and no activate intent exists (`replace` is the delivered reactivation), so §1 gains the status pill and keeps register/replace; (c) **store-column slug** — `AdminUserSummary` carries no `storefrontSlug` (detail-only) and widening the row DTO is a spec change. The password reset keeps the delivered email link (no temp-password modal) presented template-style. The admin may not import a merchant module, so F01 creates one admin-local `SegmentedControl` — the template keeps it page-local too (`pages/admin/shared.tsx`); the merchant twin in `catalog-fields.tsx` is untouched and consolidation is a follow-up.

## Áreas afetadas

## Ordem e paralelismo

1. **Onda 1:** F01, which publishes `admin-controls.tsx`.
2. **Onda 2:** F02 and F03 in parallel — disjoint write sets, no logical dependency between them; **onda 3:** F04 over the integrated result.
3. **Integração:** the main agent validates ownership with `pop_check_scope.py`, integrates and then checks the `agent` criteria.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** a front concludes a criterion needs `src/auth/**`, a byte-frozen route, `src/data-directory/**` or `src/components/ui/**` — mitigação: it stops and reports `BLOCKED`; the gap is recorded, never widened into a service or DTO change.
- **Risco:** existing suites assert the current markup and the `/admin?success=created` location — mitigação: no test is written or run here; the realignment is registered as the phase checklist for [[14.4.4-phase-verification]].
- **Risco:** `src/app/globals.css` is also touched by 14.4.2 (`.admin-dashboard__*`) — mitigação: F02 removes exactly one rule (`.admin-confirm-actions`); `.admin-account*` stays, because its consumer is the V1 order list (`src/app/orders/order-views.tsx`), not this family.
- **Abortar se:** the create-redirect decision is rejected, or a delivered notice cannot reach its section without a new route contract — sinalizar `blocked: true` with route file and line as evidence.
