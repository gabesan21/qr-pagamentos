# 15.1.5-create-missing-parity-current-route-records — Create missing parity current-route records

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.1]]
- **Plano:** [[15.1.5-create-missing-parity-current-route-records.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.1.5-create-missing-parity-current-route-records.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.1.5-create-missing-parity-current-route-records.verify]]

## O quê

Add the three missing `current-route` parity records for live routes that have none — `src/app/(merchant)/error.tsx`, `src/app/admin/orders/v2/[id]/loading.tsx` and `src/app/(merchant)/orders/v2/[id]/loading.tsx` — through a sanctioned, fail-closed procedure of the parity checker (never a hand edit of `manifest.json`/`obligations.ndjson`), each with its route, `routeKind`, disposition, owner and evidence target, and rebind the manifest so the canonical `pnpm frontend-parity:check` passes. Nothing else changes: no V1 removal, no webhook, no checkout behavior, no migration.

## Por quê

The canonical parity gate compares live routes with mapped records; three unmapped routes make it fail today and would block 15.1.3's refresh of the contract after the V1 deletion.
