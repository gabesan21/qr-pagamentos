### Brief para decisão

- **Entrega:** make a registered currency pair *earn* the right to be selected — a documented pre-order provider probe plus durable, observed PIX/BRL evidence from the first real order — and make `GlobalPaymentSettings` (BRL/PIX) a refusal the checkout actually applies before dispatch.
- **Estratégia:** **What the in-repo documentation proves (the only source; no external or invented fact).** The **quote is the only documented pre-order test of a pair**: `POST /pricing/panel/buy` takes exactly `currency_uuid` + `exchange_currency_uuid` + one amount (`raw/pricing/calculate-buy-conversion-panel.md:11-18`) and answers `200` or the closed codes `validation.exchange_currency_invalid` (400), `validation.currency_not_found…
- **Topologia:** `pop-execution-orchestrator` over three `pop-executor` fronts in two waves — three disjoint write sets (provider/schema, services+UI, checkout+contracts) and a real dependency on F01; **`ui-change` applies to F02** (merchant/admin surfaces) and the coordinator should stamp it on the card's 004/005 rows. **Perfis:** fixos por papel nativo; `size: L` limita orçamento, não topologia.
- **Risco principal:** treating a passing probe as PIX/BRL proof (L2) — mitigation: the gate is named *reachability*; the only PIX/BRL claim in code comes from an observed order, and the settings check is fail-closed.
