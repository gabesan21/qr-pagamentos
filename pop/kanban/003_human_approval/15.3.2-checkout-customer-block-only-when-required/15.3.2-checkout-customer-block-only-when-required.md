# 15.3.2-checkout-customer-block-only-when-required — Checkout customer block only when required

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.3]]
- **Plano:** [[15.3.2-checkout-customer-block-only-when-required.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.3.2-checkout-customer-block-only-when-required.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.3.2-checkout-customer-block-only-when-required.verify]]

## O quê

When the owner's checkout data policy is `NONE`, `/pay/[identifier]` and `/store/[slug]/pay` render no customer heading, no `Alert`/notice and no field group; the pay action and the privacy line keep their position, and `checkoutNoCustomerData` is deleted from both dictionaries.

## Por quê

Decision 4 and finding A13: the template hides the whole block (`needsForm`); the app announces that no data is required.
