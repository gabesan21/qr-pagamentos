# Plano — [[15.3.2-checkout-customer-block-only-when-required]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** with checkout data policy `NONE`, both buyer surfaces render no customer block at all — no customer heading, no `Alert role="status"` notice, no field group holding zero customer fields — and the now-unused dictionary key disappears.
- **Resultado observável:** on `/pay/[identifier]` with `NONE` the card shows only the pay action (plus error/unavailable alerts when they fire) in the same position and spacing as a policy with fields; on `/store/[slug]/pay` with `NONE` the amount field, the pay action, the privacy line and the return link keep their exact place and the notice is gone. `checkoutNoCustomerData` no longer exists in either dictionary.

## Estratégia

The template is the reference: `needsForm` (`docs/template/app/src/pages/checkout/CheckoutPage.tsx:416,500-551`) gates the entire buyer fieldset, while the submit button and privacy line sit **outside** it and never move. Mirror that shape — do not replace the notice with another placeholder and do not reorder the card.

The two surfaces are asymmetric and must not be unified here. On `/pay` the card header is `checkoutCustomerHeading`, so with `NONE` the header and the customer field group are both suppressed and the alerts + submit button keep their order and grid spacing inside the card content. On `/store/[slug]/pay` the card header is `storefrontPayHeading` and the amount field is always required, so the header, the field group and the footer stay untouched: only the `role="status"` notice line goes. Keep `checkoutCustomerHeading` in the dictionaries — it is still used by the non-`NONE` path and by the owner order detail.

The dictionary key is deleted only after confirming zero remaining references: its other consumers (`src/app/pay/[identifier]/public-checkout-form.tsx`, `src/app/orders/order-views.tsx`) are V1 modules removed by 15.1.1, and this task depends on 15.1.4. Tests are **written, never run** here (the run belongs to 15.3.4). No contract changes: the spec already says the checkout renders exactly the policy-required fields.
