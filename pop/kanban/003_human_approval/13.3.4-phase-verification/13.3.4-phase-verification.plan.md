# Plano — [[13.3.4-phase-verification]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** run Phase 13.3's accumulated checklist on the integrated 13.3.1→13.3.3 tree, repair only Phase 13.3 defects, and hand the epoch off to the human with a consolidated verification checklist, a closing report and a **suggested** `develop` → `main` PR.
- **Resultado observável:** every `phase` criterion of the three sibling plans has a recorded run with its exact output; two new notes in `pop/notes/references/` let the human verify and merge Epoch 13 without reopening any card; no PR is opened by the agent.

## Estratégia

Planned **before** the sibling tasks execute, from their plans in `003_human_approval`. The checklist below is therefore a **forecast**: the first act of 004 is to re-read the three final plans and their memory ledgers and to **amend by `lacuna`** any criterion that moved — a sibling plan that gained a criterion at its own gate wins over this table, and a criterion that disappeared is recorded as such, never silently dropped.

**Nothing runs through Docker.** Every gate here is direct pnpm on the integrated tree. `db:contract-check` is static (`migration-policy.mjs` + `db-contract-check.mjs`, no database); `db:test`, `install/test.sh`, `container:*` and the evidence runners stay `user` and go to the human checklist. In a fresh worktree install with `SHARP_IGNORE_GLOBAL_LIBVIPS=1 pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` on the pinned Node; if a pnpm wrapper misbehaves, fall back to `./node_modules/.bin/{tsc,vitest,next}` and record the substitution ([[pop/notes/references/limites-de-verificacao|verification limits]]). A new blocker of that kind is appended there, one line with task and evidence.

**Repair reach is Phase 13.3 only.** A falsification traceable to 13.3.1/13.3.2/13.3.3's own diff is repaired here; a durable-contract defect or an unmet request from an earlier phase is **not** — it is recorded in memory and in the report's follow-ups as a modification/task proposal (WORKFLOW, "Verificação de phase"). Phase 13.4 is blocked on the `nautt-exchange-currencies-contract` research; while it stays blocked this task is the epoch's hand-off and must say so explicitly.

The two notes are the deliverable the human actually consumes, in the Epoch 15 mould ([[pop/notes/references/2026-09-23-epoch-15-report|report]] · [[pop/notes/references/2026-09-23-epoch-15-human-verification-checklist|checklist]]): the checklist consolidates **every `verify: user` item of the whole epoch** — 13.1.1 #8 (first production deliveries accepted, zero `webhook.rejected`), 13.2.1 #8/#9 (`pnpm db:test` race, buyer capture), 13.2.2 #10 (clean install with loopback and with a real host, `install/test.sh`), 13.2.3 #9 (rotation rehearsal, then TOTP login and a Nautt credential read), 13.3.2 C8 (shell footer and locale line at 1280px and 375px), 13.3.3 #10 (execute the rehearsal protocol and fill a dated evidence section) — plus the permanent Docker-exclusive items, each row carrying its exact step and expected pass.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** the forecast checklist diverges from what the siblings actually delivered — mitigação: A0 re-reads the final plans first; divergence is a `lacuna` amendment, appended, never a renumbering.
- **Risco:** a gate fails for a defect rooted before Phase 13.3 (the `admin:source-check` debt predates the epoch), or a repair silently widens `owns` — mitigação: classify before repairing, send out-of-reach findings to the report's follow-ups, and stop for the principal's authorization before any out-of-`owns` edit, recording it as its own memory entry (15.4.3 precedent).
- **Abortar se:** a sibling ledger is missing, the tree does not contain all three merged diffs, or a criterion would require Docker — sinalizar `blocked: true` naming the path or the command.
