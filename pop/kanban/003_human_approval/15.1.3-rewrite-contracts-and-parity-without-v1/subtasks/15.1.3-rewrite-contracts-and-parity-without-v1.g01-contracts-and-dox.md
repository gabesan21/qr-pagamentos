## Contrato de execução

**The rewrite is subtractive and de-versioning, never re-derivation.** A statement is *retained* when a V2 section incorporates it by reference ("mirrors V1 verbatim", "reuses `CustomerSnapshotV1` unchanged", "the settlement map V1 is unchanged"): it stops being "the V1 rule that V2 copies" and becomes the single normative rule, losing only its era suffix in prose. A statement is *deleted* when it is scoped to an artefact 15.1.1 removed.

In `checkout-and-order-lifecycle.md`:

- **Retain and de-version:** owner/policy/public-access requirements; the customer snapshot, its normalization, the Brazil address snapshot and the exposure fences (keep the identifier `CustomerSnapshotV1` — it is a live type name); the public checkout mutation contract, its reservation/replay/capability/dispatch discipline and its response/outcome matrix, now bound to the V2 tables and services the 9.3.1 section already pins; single-use consumption, expiry and concurrency; the settlement map and the versioned reconciliation CAS.
- **Delete:** the repeated `Updated` changelog (one line naming 15.1.3 replaces it; the history belongs to this task's memory entries); the "Implemented slices" bullets for tasks 4.3.1/4.2.2, which describe deleted modules; every "no V1 row is mutated / V1 guarantees unchanged / V1-first branch resolves first / V1 table untouched / byte-frozen V1 ledger or legacy section" fence inside the V2 sections; the "both eras" framing of 14.6.1 (one composition remains); the directory gap about "no unified V1+V2 list"; the "Open" bullet on V1 settle wiring and the 9.2.1 sentence deferring it.
- **Keep the section order and headings** of the surviving V2 sections; do not merge, reorder or re-argue them.

In `catalog-and-payment-links.md` and `administrative-foundation.md`, remove only V1-scoped sentences and clauses. Where a V2 rule is stated as "exactly the V1 grammar/semantics" (identifier grammar and namespace, expiry evaluation, single-use type), restate the rule itself so the contract survives the removal of its referent.

In `storefront-and-customization.md` and `application-frontend-system.md` (round-1 delta), the same rule applies to the payment-link era only: drop the "V1 `checkoutPolicy` precedent" framing (the `checkoutDataPolicy` exposure stays, stated on its own terms), the "V1 link-driven `products` list no longer renders" clauses and their frozen-DTO fences, the "retained legacy links" wording, and the "V1 compatibility rule" item of the frontend guardrail list. These specs also carry **storefront-owned** version labels (`Logo V1`, `Settings workspace V1`, `Slug/Display-name/Accent-color V1`, `Public page and browser cart V1`) that have nothing to do with the removed era: de-version the label where it is only a label, never delete the contract it names, and list in the report every `V1` token left standing.

In `src/checkout/AGENTS.md`, drop the `public-checkout-presentation.ts` V1 DTO line entirely and rewrite the `checkout_attempt_v2` line so it describes the checkout/status routes and the `/pay/[identifier]` read without the V1-first branch language; keep the standalone and cart lines. In the root `AGENTS.md`, no V1 statement was found: verify, change nothing else, and never add a line (ceiling).
