# 15.2.3-payment-method-visibility-and-public-dto-trim — Payment method visibility and public DTO trim

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.2]]
- **Plano:** [[15.2.3-payment-method-visibility-and-public-dto-trim.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.2.3-payment-method-visibility-and-public-dto-trim.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.2.3-payment-method-visibility-and-public-dto-trim.verify]]

## O quê

Adapter failures log the documented Nautt `code` redacted (never body, key or secret); the owner order detail shows the provider `paymentMethod`; `pixQrCodeUrl` leaves the public checkout/status DTOs and the client parsers.

## Por quê

Provider errors are opaque in logs (A7), the merchant cannot see the payment method behind an order, and the public DTO carries a field no view renders (A8).
