# 15.4.1-migrate-template-vocabulary — Migrate template vocabulary

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/15-v2-only-pix-integrity-and-explicit-checkout|Phase 15.4]]
- **Plano:** [[15.4.1-migrate-template-vocabulary.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[15.4.1-migrate-template-vocabulary.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[15.4.1-migrate-template-vocabulary.verify]]

## O quê

Migrate the authenticated and public surfaces still written in the legacy shadcn vocabulary (`text-muted-foreground`, `bg-card`, `rounded-lg`, `font-[family-name:var(--font-display)]`, `bg-primary` in accent roles, ad-hoc `max-w-[var(...)]`) to the declared template utilities (`text-text-2/3`, `bg-surface`, `rounded-card`, `shadow-card`, `font-display`, `bg-accent`, `max-w-app/checkout/auth-form`) wherever the template maps the screen, and resolve the `admin:source-check` raw-control/local-variant findings in `src/app/admin/**` and `language-switcher.tsx` (follow-up F6/C5 of 15.3.4) by composing the owned primitives instead of local variants. No business, authorization, redaction or exact-decimal behavior changes; the public checkout (already converged by 15.3.3) is only re-checked, not redesigned.

## Por quê

DESIGN.md declares one vocabulary but the code carries two (~180 legacy uses in 40 files against 21 converged files), so the console tone is inconsistent and the shared-UI inventory gate reports drift.
