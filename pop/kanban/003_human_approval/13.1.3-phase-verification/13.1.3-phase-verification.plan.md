## Objective and expected result

- **Objective:** prove Phase 13.1 on the integrated tree — the restored HMAC gate holds under the suites and no `BETA(M-5.1)` caveat survives anywhere normative — and repair only what the reversal itself falsified.
- **Observable result:** `pnpm check` is green with the webhook suites executed and none skipped; `webhook-intake-beta.test.ts` is absent; the `BETA(M-5.1)`/`resolveOwner` sweeps return zero; the first-production-delivery check is stated for the human, not simulated locally.

## Strategy

Planned ahead of its dependencies by human command, from the sibling plans in 003. Only one `verify: phase` criterion accumulated — 13.1.1's C10 ("`pnpm check` and the Nautt webhook suites pass after the reversal"); 13.1.2 declared no phase or user criterion, and 13.1.1's C8 is the phase's single `verify: user` item. The plan therefore adds the two re-checks that only exist once the two diffs sit together: the sweeps of 13.1.1 C2/C6 and 13.1.2 C1 re-run over the **integrated** tree, and the `401` protocol is read end to end across intake, runtime and route.

**Entry precondition, checked first:** the ledgers `pop/memory/<date>/13.1.1-restore-webhook-hmac-verification.md` and `…/13.1.2-clear-beta-caveats-and-close-reversal-gate.md` exist, and the **final** plans of both siblings are re-read (they may have been amended at 003/005). If execution diverged from the plans this checklist was built from — a criterion changed, a write set widened, a new suite appeared — report it and let the principal return this plan by `lacuna`; do not silently verify a different contract.

Commands are direct pnpm, never a container. The pinned Node in `.node-version` is the reference; when the local Node differs, pnpm aborts on its deps check (A12 of [[pop/researches/pix-checkout-review/2026-09-22-review-findings|the pix-checkout review]]) and the equivalents `./node_modules/.bin/tsc --noEmit`, `./node_modules/.bin/vitest run`, `./node_modules/.bin/next build` plus `pnpm lint`'s chain are the accepted form. Same for `SHARP_IGNORE_GLOBAL_LIBVIPS=1 pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` ([[pop/notes/references/limites-de-verificacao|verification limits]]). **Setup is not a criterion attempt:** switching to the bin form or fixing an install costs no attempt.

Run the targeted webhook suites before the aggregate gate, so a reversal defect is diagnosed on a cheap signal: `src/integrations/nautt/webhook-intake.test.ts`, `webhook-signature.test.ts`, `webhook-delivery-store.test.ts`, `webhook-delivery-recovery.test.ts`, `webhook-delivery-history-port.test.ts`, `src/app/api/nautt/webhooks/route.test.ts` and the new `src/observability/webhook-rejection-log.test.ts` that 13.1.1 creates. Then `pnpm check` whole (typecheck, lint, test, build) — the phase does not close on a focused run.

**Repair reach is the falsification, not the design.** Assertions, fixtures and mocks that the reversal invalidated are repaired here (including `route.test.ts`, which 13.1.1 could not edit, and a leftover beta caveat, removable by deletion only). A defect in the durable contract — the gate ordering wrong, a record member that leaks, a status the spec does not describe — is **not** fixed here: it is recorded in this task's memory as a follow-up proposal (modification or new task), per the phase-verification rule of [[WORKFLOW|WORKFLOW]]. No new verification infrastructure is built to make a criterion pass.

## Risks and abort conditions

- **Risk:** a suite fails on environment (sandbox, Node pin, flakiness), not on a defect — mitigation: **at most two attempts** per criterion; on the second, record `ambiente` in the telemetry, reclassify the criterion as `verify: user` with a Log line and move on. Never build infrastructure to make it pass.
- **Risk:** the repair drifts into redesigning the gate — mitigation: a failure whose remedy changes the contract stops being a repair and becomes a memory-recorded follow-up.
- **Risk:** execution diverged from the plans this checklist anticipated — mitigation: report for a `lacuna` amendment; append-only, never a rewrite of criteria IDs.
- **Abort if:** either ledger is missing, or a criterion can only pass through Docker/production — `blocked: true` naming the file/criterion.
