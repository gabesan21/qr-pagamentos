# Plan — [[12.2.4-rebuild-design-system-specimen]]

- **Stage:** 002_planning · **Owner:** agent planner

## Objective and expected result

- **Objective:** rebuild `/design-system` as the closed, role-neutral specimen for every production shared primitive/composition and every applicable inventory state, across the six stored themes, `pt-BR`/`en`, responsive modes, real keyboard focus and reduced motion.
- **Observable result:** one unprefixed server-first route makes the delivered visual foundation inspectable without domain behavior, while fresh fail-closed evidence binds each fixture/state to its production owner and parity authority before any route family migrates.

## Strategy

Use `src/components/ui/inventory.json`, the primitive foundation named by `DESIGN.md`, and `DataDirectory` as the closed source set. Add a specimen-local executable coverage map from public owner/state to deterministic fixture/probe; compose production owners without forking or restyling them, and mark inventory N/A states instead of fabricating behavior. Canonical identity/assets are project-owned delivered inputs, not a rights gate.

Keep specimen/demo fixtures under `src/app/design-system/`: inert formatted/redacted data, no business DTO/service, auth capability, real mutation target, mock store, route prefix or product-route import. The page remains server-first; only owner-required interactions use narrow client islands. The legacy `[lang]/design-system` 404 stays unchanged.

Evidence derives 48 base entries (six themes × two locales × 320/375/768/1440) and interaction/state probes from the coverage map. Locale selection follows the existing unprefixed preference contract; theme/state setup is deterministic. Bind hashes for parity, coverage, owners, dictionaries, tokens, fonts, assets, test/verifier and captures; no direct template route exists, so never invent a raster golden—trace fixtures to the reachable component obligations and current-only `/design-system` record.

## Affected areas

- `src/app/design-system/**`, `src/i18n/dictionaries/design-system/**` — specimen-only composition, fixtures and bilingual copy.
- `tests/design-system.evidence.spec.ts`, verifier/checker scripts, `artifacts/design-system/**`, parity generated authority — closed runtime/source evidence.
- `DESIGN.md`, `src/components/ui/AGENTS.md`, `pop/specs/application-frontend-system.md` — delivered specimen/evidence truth.

## Risks and abort conditions

- Abort if an inventory-declared owner/state is absent or incompatible: report the dependency gap; do not repair `src/components/ui/`, `src/data-directory/`, tokens, brand, shell or a product route in this task.
- Fail closed on missing/duplicate/stale coverage, source/hash drift, external requests, cross-locale leakage, hidden/obscured focus, horizontal overflow, motion-dependent meaning, serious accessibility findings, or any evidence not produced at the task HEAD.
- Planning preflight passed the direct shared-inventory checker; `pnpm frontend-parity:check` could not start because this `/tmp` worktree's pnpm store SQLite index was unavailable. Re-run in the task worktree; repeated failure is an execution-environment blocker, not a waiver.
