## Front notes

- The mount belongs in the root layout, which already resolves the locale and can therefore pass the localized region label; `ToastViewport` needs a `label` string, and `src/i18n/dictionaries.ts` is `server-only`, so no client file may import a dictionary — every string arrives as a prop.
- The bridge takes a **closed registry of already-localized notices** (`param`, `value`, kind, message) plus the params to strip. Pages pass the entry they already resolve today; the root layout passes the two global `language` entries, whose copy already exists as `languageSaved`/`languageError` in the shared dictionary. That layout mount is what keeps the widened locale return (F02) visible on pages that render no language banner.
- After raising the toast the bridge strips its params with `history.replaceState`, so a refresh does not re-toast. `useSearchParams` needs a Suspense boundary; provide one at the mount site.
- Keep the fallback `<noscript>`-only, wrapping the Alert that renders today with its current `role` and variant. A visually-hidden live region would double-announce against sonner's own region and violates the frontend spec's live-semantics rule.
- The five mechanical sites are exactly: `ProductNotice` and `CategoryNotice` (`catalog-notices.tsx`, rendered from `catalog/page.tsx` and `catalog/categories/page.tsx`), `PaymentLinkV2Notice` (`links/page.tsx`), `OrderV2Notice` (`orders/page.tsx`) and the inline Alert in `admin/page.tsx`. Their notice values are already closed enums resolved server-side — that is what makes the mapping mechanical. Do not touch any other notice.
- Reduced motion is already enforced globally with `!important` in `globals.css`; do not add motion CSS and do not edit that file.
- `DESIGN.md` records, in its "Accessibility and feedback" subsection only, exactly what the plan's Strategy dictates: top-right stack, 5s success auto-dismiss, sticky error/warning with optional retry, motion inside the existing reduced-motion collapse.
- The five page tests assert the current banners; update them to the new composition. Do not run them — verification is `phase` (14.2.5).
