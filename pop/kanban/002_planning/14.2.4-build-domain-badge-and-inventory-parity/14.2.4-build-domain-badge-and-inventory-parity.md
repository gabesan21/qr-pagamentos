# 14.2.4-build-domain-badge-and-inventory-parity — Build domain badge and inventory parity


- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/14-template-fidelity-convergence|Phase 14.2]]
- **Plano:** [[14.2.4-build-domain-badge-and-inventory-parity.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[14.2.4-build-domain-badge-and-inventory-parity.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[14.2.4-build-domain-badge-and-inventory-parity.verify]]

## O quê

The shared inventory reaches template parity: domain badge families (`ProviderStateBadge`, `LocalOutcomeBadge`, `LinkLifecycleBadge`, `AccountStateBadge`, `EntityStateBadge`) with soft tints and dot per the template's `StatusBadge.tsx`, an owned `ImageUploader` (drop tile, staged preview, remove), a compact `CopyField` variant, `Monogram` 48px on accent-soft, `QrDisplay` generating the QR client-side with the identity centre-cut, and a `StatCard` sparkline; `inventory.json` and the shared-UI check reflect the additions.

## Por quê

Callers re-derive badge tones by hand (drifting between pages), there is no uploader owner, and the template's numeric/identity components are approximations — every family convergence would otherwise rebuild them locally.
