## Contrato de execução

- The local `paymentTone`/`paymentCopy` map goes away: provider states render through `ProviderStateBadge` and the owned tone map (`refunded` neutral), never a page-local tone.
- The unavailable view stops being `Card` + destructive `Alert` and adopts the same `EmptyState` vocabulary as the storefront and the checkout, keeping the return-to-store affordance and the single opaque cause.
- Delete from `globals.css` only rules whose last referrer is gone, proving each with a search over `src/**` before removal: `.receipt-rail*`, the public `.storefront-*` set (shell, rail, heading, introduction, products, card, price, group, product-*, stepper, custom-amount, cart, error, skeleton, logo, pay-footer and the cart `[data-slot="empty"]` override) and the seven `.checkout-*` rules 14.6.1 preserved (`card`, `form`, `payment`, `qr`, `copy`, `privacy`, `checkout-v2__total`). `.storefront-workspace*`, `.storefront-logo-block|current|actions|staged` and `.storefront-preview*` belong to merchant settings and stay for 14.5.3/14.7.1.
- Keep the page's `data-theme-preview` + `--storefront-accent` mechanism: its own allowlisted declaration verbatim, or delegation to 14.6.1's shell. `scripts/check-design-tokens.mjs` is path-bound and this front may not edit it.
- Do not reintroduce what 14.6.1 excluded by DTO contract (expiry countdown, single-use badge, order reference); creating a new shared component or touching `inventory.json` is out of scope.
- Spec and `DESIGN.md` receive lines, not narration: one `Task 14.6.2` entry in the storefront spec's "Implemented slices", the minimal amendment to any requirement bullet the diff falsified, and `DESIGN.md` "Public payment and storefront surfaces" updated to the converged shell and states.
