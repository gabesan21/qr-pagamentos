# Plan — [[14.6.2-converge-storefront-and-standalone-pay]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** make `/store/[slug]` and `/store/[slug]/pay` speak the checkout language converged by [[14.6.1-converge-public-checkout]] — same merchant header/rail (logo or `Monogram` + display name + trust line), same `EmptyState` unavailable/empty/error vocabulary, domain badge tones, public footer with `LanguageSwitcher` — with the standalone page consuming 14.6.1's payment-phase controller and outcome views instead of its local fork, and the public `storefront-*`/`receipt-rail*` BEM retired from `globals.css` in favour of the projected utilities.
- **Resultado observável:** a buyer walking storefront → standalone pay → `/pay/[identifier]` sees one identity, one status vocabulary and one furniture set in both locales; `globals.css` keeps no public storefront BEM block, while cart, money, issuance and sessionless behaviour stay identical.

## Estratégia

Presentation only, with every public contract frozen: sessionless routes and rate limits, the browser-local cart (key/envelope, 1–9,999 quantities, recovery notice, exact BigInt per-currency totals never summed), the product-only cart-checkout body with its `201` clear-and-redirect, one-time link issuance, the standalone submit body `{ idempotencyKey, amount, customer }` with policy-exact fields, capability polling and the single opaque unavailable outcome. A convergence that would require touching any of them stops and is reported, never adapted.

The task consumes; it does not invent. 14.6.1 owns the shared rail, footer, privacy furniture, `EmptyState` vocabulary, payment-phase controller, outcome views and the checkout copy behind them; this task imports them and deletes what they replace. No new shared component and no `inventory.json` edit — a genuine gap returns to the agente principal instead of producing a second rail, tone map or state machine. The audit's tone drift (`REFUNDED` danger here, neutral in the checkout) dies by rendering provider states through the owned `ProviderStateBadge`. Retirement is scoped by consumer, not by prefix: only the blocks the two public routes stop using leave `globals.css` (`.receipt-rail*` and the public `.storefront-*` set), while `.storefront-workspace*`, `.storefront-logo-block|current|actions|staged` and `.storefront-preview*` serve merchant settings and stay for 14.5.3/14.7.1. Replacements use the projected utilities only — `pnpm lint` runs `check-design-tokens`, which rejects raw hex/px/rem/ch and any inline style outside its path-bound allowlist, so both pages keep their `data-theme-preview` + `--storefront-accent` declaration verbatim and neither front edits that script. Layouts `boxed|table` survive as utility compositions, both locales change together, and execution is sequential: storefront page plus CSS/copy retirement first, standalone page plus the durable contract lines second.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** 14.6.1 leaves the payment-phase/outcome views welded to the V1/V2 DTOs — mitigação: F02 reporta `BLOCKED` com evidência; duplicar a máquina de estados está fora do contrato.
- **Risco:** removing a `storefront-*` block still used by merchant settings breaks that surface — mitigação: só sai a lista pública; `workspace`/`logo-*`/`preview*` permanecem.
- **Risco:** utility conversion reintroduces raw visual values, an inline style or copy in one locale only — mitigação: `check-design-tokens` dentro do `pnpm lint` e tipos dos dicionários no `pnpm typecheck`.
- **Abortar se:** a convergência exigir mudar body, conjunto de status, rate limit, redirect ou redação de rota pública — sinalizar `blocked: true` com path e linha do contrato afetado.
