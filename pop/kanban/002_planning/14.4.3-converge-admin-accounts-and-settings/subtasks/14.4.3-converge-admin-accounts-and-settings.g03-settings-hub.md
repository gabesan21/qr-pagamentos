## Contrato de execução

- The section nav becomes a scroll-spy: reuse the delivered merchant pattern (`IntersectionObserver` over the section ids, `aria-current` on the active anchor, `lg`-only nav, unchanged `#sec-*` ids and anchors). Keep it an anchor list so it works without JS.
- **§4 becomes functional.** The route and service already validate and persist, and the runtime role holds `SELECT` + `UPDATE(currencies, payment_methods, updated_at)` on the singleton — the inertness was UI-only. Render the switches as real checkbox controls named `currencies`/`paymentMethods` with values `BRL`/`PIX`, enable Save, and require the owned `ConfirmDialog` before turning a row off (the closed catalog has one value per family). Never invent extra catalog values.
- **§1 keeps its redaction fence.** `listMappings` is administrator-redacted to `{code,label}` and active-only, and no activate intent exists — `replace` is the delivered reactivation. Deliver the status pill (`StatusBadge`) and keep register/replace-or-reactivate; the Nautt/exchange UUID columns and inactive rows stay recorded gaps (the pair UUIDs already render in §2). Do not add a store or service read.
- Per-section feedback: the four unambiguous outcomes (`success=exchange-currency`, `success|error=settings`, `theme-default`, and the `language=saved|error` pair the language route returns) render inside their own section as one toast through `NoticeToast` plus the same `Alert` inside `<noscript>`. `catalog-created`/`catalog-changed` cannot be attributed to §2 or §3 with the current vocabulary, so they stay page-level and the reason goes into `Resultado` as a follow-up.
- §6 applies on selection: the segmented buttons submit the existing `/language-preference` form on click (the shell switcher's `requestSubmit()` pattern), with no Save button and no route change. §5 appearance already matches the template — leave it unless a token or state defect appears.
- Every new string lands in `src/i18n/dictionaries/administration/{en,pt-BR}.ts` with both locales in the same commit.
