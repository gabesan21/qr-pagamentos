# Plano — [[14.4.3-converge-admin-accounts-and-settings]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** bring `/admin/accounts`, `/admin/accounts/[id]` and `/admin/settings` to the template's administration surface — create modal, tabbed editor with segmented/grid controls, scroll-spy settings with operable global payments — over the delivered mutations.
- **Resultado observável:** an administrator creates an account in a modal with a generated, measured and copyable password; edits one account through five tabs whose active tab survives a reload; and works `/admin/settings` with a scroll-spy nav, operable BRL/PIX switches and feedback inside the section that saved.

## Estratégia

Presentation and interaction only, composed over the delivered native POST routes. The `/admin/users/[id]/{role,status,password,delete}` routes stay byte-frozen: the UI wraps them, never re-points them. One exception is decided here — `POST /admin/users` (create, not frozen) re-points from `/admin?success=created` to `/admin/accounts?success=created|error=create-failed`, because `resolveAdminAccountsDirectoryQuery` already parses that closed notice pair, nothing produces it today, and the 14.3.2 destination rule in `DESIGN.md` requires a mutation to return to the page that submitted it.

Three template obligations are **not** reachable under that boundary and are delivered as recorded gaps, not as silent omissions: (a) **initial status** — `POST /admin/users` and `createUser` write `status: "ACTIVE"` and accept no status field, so no dead control is rendered; (b) **exchange-currency UUID columns and inactive rows** — `listMappings` is active-only and redacted to `{code,label}` by decision, and no activate intent exists (`replace` is the delivered reactivation), so §1 gains the status pill and keeps register/replace; (c) **store-column slug** — `AdminUserSummary` carries no `storefrontSlug` (detail-only), and widening the row DTO is a spec change. Each gap is written into the spec as follow-up work.

Two more constraints shape the fronts. The frozen role/status/password/delete routes land on `/admin`, which already toasts those notices — so per-save toasts on the editor cover only the routes that return to it (`?editor=`, `?reset=`). And the admin may not import a merchant module, so F01 creates one admin-local `SegmentedControl` (the template keeps it page-local in `pages/admin/shared.tsx` too); the merchant twin in `catalog-fields.tsx` is untouched and consolidation is a follow-up.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** a front concludes a criterion needs `src/auth/**`, a byte-frozen route, `src/data-directory/**` or `src/components/ui/**` — mitigação: it stops and reports `BLOCKED`; the gap is recorded, never widened into a service change.
- **Risco:** existing suites assert the current markup and the `/admin?success=created` location — mitigação: no test is written or run here; the realignment is registered as the phase checklist for [[14.4.4-phase-verification]].
- **Risco:** `src/app/globals.css` is also touched by 14.4.2 (`.admin-dashboard__*`) — mitigação: F02 removes exactly one rule (`.admin-confirm-actions`); `.admin-account*` stays because its consumer is the V1 order list (`src/app/orders/order-views.tsx`), not this family.
- **Abortar se:** the create-redirect decision is rejected by the main agent, or a delivered notice cannot reach its section without a new route contract — sinalizar `blocked: true` with the route file and line as evidence.
