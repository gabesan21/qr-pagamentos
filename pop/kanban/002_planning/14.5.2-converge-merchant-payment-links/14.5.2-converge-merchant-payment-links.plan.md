# Plano — [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** bring `/links`, `/links/new`, `/links/v2/[id]`, its `/edit` and the order drill-downs to the template's commerce model — era/currency/date filters and the currency column with legacy links merged into the same table shape, a composition form with radio cards, a searchable product picker with steppers, prices and a running total, a sticky live preview, honest edit locks, lifecycle guards with captions, a real orders summary and breadcrumbs — without touching a database, route or service contract.
- **Resultado observável:** a merchant filters links by era, currency and date, opens a legacy link in a read-only modal, builds a product-lines link seeing unit prices and the exact total update as steppers move, sees which structural fields are locked and why, cannot reactivate a settled single-use link, reads the real confirmed count and volume on the detail, and reaches every order of a link through labelled breadcrumbs.

## Estratégia

Presentation plus the narrowest additive read projections. No migration, no Prisma schema change, no new HTTP route, no change to the mutation vocabulary of `/payment-links-v2**`, and no byte of V1 link service, route or trigger behavior. Owner isolation stays absolute: every new read goes through the delivered owner-scoped services with the same opaque unavailable outcome, and no currency-pair UUID, provider field or cross-owner row enters a projection. The 14.3.2 achievements — on-page create/edit redirects and dirty-only session drafts — and the 14.3.1 toast bridge are preserved exactly; a front that breaks them fails its own criteria. `PaymentLinkV2UnavailableCard` in `link-v2-views.tsx` is **byte-frozen here**: phase 14.4 owns that composition and is integrating in parallel.

Five decisions are pinned so no executor re-decides them. **(1) Era is a partition, never a blend.** V1 links live in another table, are served by a byte-frozen list and carry no cursor, and `src/data-directory/AGENTS.md` forbids offset, totals and client-side full-list filtering — so one keyset page cannot span both eras. `era` becomes a registered enum filter (`v2` default, `legacy`), and `era=legacy` renders the owner's V1 list through the same `DataDirectory` composition with a legacy chip and a read-only modal. Dropping the delivered V1 create/revoke capability is not authorized: both stay reachable inside the legacy era, restyled into merchant-owned source, and `@/app/admin/payment-link-management` stops being imported by `/links`. **(2) Descriptions for product-line links are not feasible here.** `productLinesComposition()` writes `null` descriptions and the spec binds the bilingual description to `FIXED_AMOUNT`; exposing it would be a service and schema change. The descriptions section renders for every composition, read-only and explained for `PRODUCT_LINES`, and F04 records the divergence — nobody invents a field. **(3) The orders summary is a real read, not an aggregate surface.** Only `findForOwner` gains the confirmed count and the exact confirmed volume over the LINK orders already related in its select, plus a per-line product availability flag; the list window, the admin directory reusing this module and the row DTO stay as delivered. **(4) One exact-decimal money module.** `link-money.ts` (BigInt micro-units at the price grammar's scale, client-safe, mirroring the storefront cart module that stays untouched) is the only arithmetic on money in these files; `Number()` never touches an amount again. **(5) Lifecycle guards adapted to the derived model.** Deactivate is offered whenever the link is persisted-active (states `active`, `expired`, `paid`) — the delivered capability is preserved; activate is offered only for `inactive` and never for a settled `SINGLE_USE` link, which renders the disabled control with its caption; an `expired` link shows the caption that reopening requires editing the expiry, and says so only for `REUSABLE` — a single-use expired link stays closed. The financial lock and the new-version banner render only when a checkout attempt really exists, read through the owner prefill seam.

## Áreas afetadas

- `src/app/(merchant)/links/**` — directory, legacy era, both form flows, detail, lifecycle actions and the two order drill-downs.
- `src/auth/payment-link-v2-view.ts`, `src/auth/payment-link-v2-prefill.ts` — registered filters, window predicates and additive owner-only members; `src/i18n/dictionaries/payment-links-directory/*` plus a new `payment-links-form/*` domain, `DESIGN.md` and `pop/specs/catalog-and-payment-links.md` — copy in both locales and the durable record.

## Frentes de execução

### F01 — Directory, legacy era and shared foundations

- **Entrega:** `/links` with era/currency/from/to filters, the currency column and the template column set; the legacy era as `DataDirectory` rows with a read-only modal and the restyled V1 operations; the projection and `link-money.ts` that F02 and F03 consume.
- **Contrato:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.g01-directory-and-legacy]] — *siga como fatia única desta frente* · **Papel:** `pop-executor` · **Depends on:** nenhuma · **Critérios:** C01–C04, C11.

### F02 — Create and edit composition form

- **Entrega:** `/links/new` and `/links/v2/[id]/edit` with radio cards, searchable picker with steppers and prices, exact running total, expiration clear, sticky preview, inline errors, honest locks and the conditional new-version banner.
- **Contrato:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.g02-link-form]] — *siga como fatia única desta frente* · **Papel:** `pop-executor` · **Depends on:** F01 (`link-money.ts`) · **Critérios:** C05–C07, C11.

### F03 — Detail, lifecycle actions and order drill-downs

- **Entrega:** `/links/v2/[id]` with the action row under the header, guarded lifecycle controls with captions, the real orders summary, unavailable-product badges and exact totals; both drill-downs with breadcrumbs, `CopyField` ids, payer column and domain badges.
- **Contrato:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.g03-detail-and-orders]] — *siga como fatia única desta frente* · **Papel:** `pop-executor` · **Depends on:** F01 (projection, `link-money.ts`, dictionary domain) · **Critérios:** C04, C08–C11.

### F04 — Contracts and evidence

- **Entrega:** `DESIGN.md`, the catalog spec and the touched DOX telling the truth about the era partition, the new projection members, the locked description section and the guard adaptation, plus the phase checklist for 14.5.4.
- **Contrato:** [[14.5.2-converge-merchant-payment-links.g04-contracts]] — *siga como fatia única desta frente* · **Papel:** `pop-executor` · **Depends on:** F01, F02, F03 (integrated diff) · **Critérios:** C12.

## Ordem e paralelismo

1. **Onda 1:** F01 (fundações: projeção, filtros, `link-money.ts`, domínio `payment-links-directory`).
2. **Onda 2:** F02 e F03 em paralelo — write sets disjuntos; F02 cria o domínio `payment-links-form`, F03 herda `payment-links-directory` já liberado por F01.
3. **Onda 3:** F04 sobre o diff integrado. **Integração:** o agente principal valida ownership com `pop/scripts/pop_check_scope.py`, confere que `PaymentLinkV2UnavailableCard` está intacto e roda os critérios `agent`.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** conflito com a phase 14.4, que integra em paralelo sobre `link-v2-views.tsx` — mitigação: C10 congela a composição indisponível e mantém estáveis os helpers exportados que o admin importa. **Risco:** widening de tipo em serviço com dublê de teste existente quebra a compilação — mitigação: F01/F02 podem ajustar dublês **apenas** mecanicamente (membro novo), sem caso ou asserção nova; suíte é assunto de 14.5.4 (C13).
- **Abortar se:** um controle do template exigir schema, rota ou mutação nova — sinalizar `blocked: true` com o controle, o dado ausente e o arquivo consultado.
