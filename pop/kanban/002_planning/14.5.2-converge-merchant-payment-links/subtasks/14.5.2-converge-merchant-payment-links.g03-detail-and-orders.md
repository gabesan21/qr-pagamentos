## Contrato de execução

- **Shared file discipline:** `link-v2-views.tsx` is also consumed by the administrator detail. Keep every exported helper's name and signature (`formatLinkInstant`, `linkStateLabel`, `linkTypeLabel`, `linkKindLabel`, `linkSummary`, `copyLabels`, `LinkStateBadge`, `PaymentLinkV2DetailCard`), keep the optional `owner`/`showShareUrl` props working, and leave `PaymentLinkV2UnavailableCard` byte-identical — phase 14.4 owns it and integrates in parallel.
- **Action row** moves directly under the header badge row: open checkout, edit, new version, view orders, then the lifecycle control. **Guards:** deactivate is offered whenever the link is persisted-active (derived `active`, `expired` or `paid`); activate is offered only for `inactive`, and a settled `SINGLE_USE` link renders it disabled with the settled caption; an `expired` link renders the caption saying that reopening requires editing the expiry, offered only for `REUSABLE`. Keep the confirmation dialog and the delivered action/version CAS payload untouched.
- **Orders summary** uses the real confirmed count and confirmed volume from the projection F01 delivers — the hardcoded `confirmed: 0, volume: "0.00"` disappears. **Composition table** shows unit price, line total and subtotal computed with `link-money.ts`; a line whose product is unavailable carries the warning badge next to its title.
- **Drill-downs:** `/links/v2/[id]/orders` gains the 3-level breadcrumb (links › identifier › orders) and its order detail the 4-level one; identifiers render as `CopyField`; the row column shows the payer from the delivered policy-exact snapshot instead of the description alone; provider state, local outcome and link lifecycle use `ProviderStateBadge`, `LocalOutcomeBadge` and `LinkLifecycleBadge` with dictionary labels. Absent payer facts are labelled as not provided instead of omitted, and the float line total in the drill-down detail is replaced with exact arithmetic.
- **Unavailable state** of the drill-down aligns with the `EmptyState` composition already used by the orders family; do not invent a new illustration and do not touch the links-family card owned by 14.4.
- No mutation, route, service or DTO change: every read stays the delivered owner-scoped service with its one opaque unavailable outcome, and the `filter.link` force-binding of the drill-down keeps its exact server-side semantics.
- Every new or changed user-facing string lives in `payment-links-directory/{en,pt-BR}.ts` under the same key.
