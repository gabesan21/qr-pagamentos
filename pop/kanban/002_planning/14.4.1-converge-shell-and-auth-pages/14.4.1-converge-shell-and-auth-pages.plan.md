# Plano — [[14.4.1-converge-shell-and-auth-pages]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** converge the authenticated shell and the shared access pages (login, MFA challenge, reset, 404) to the template journeys, changing presentation and client interaction only.
- **Resultado observável:** the top bar names the current route; the rail becomes a drawer at 1024px and ends in monogram/username/role pill/`by Nautt Finance`; a merchant with an enabled store reaches it from the top bar; login shows the leading brand panel with tagline, strip and six swatches, a forgot-password disclosure, show/hide and inline required copy; the challenge is six OTP cells with paste, auto-submit and Back; the reset page carries requirement hint, strength meter, mismatch feedback, distinct unusable-link states and its own success; the 404 speaks both locales.

## Estratégia

Shell and login run first because each owns a stylesheet surface; reset and 404 compose the auth card login delivers; contracts close last. Four decisions constrain execution. (1) Presentation and client interaction only — no service, guard, session or authorization change; the single route edit is the reset success redirect target. (2) The reset service cannot distinguish invalid, expired and used: `validateResetChallenge` returns `ResetUserReference | null` and `findValidToken` folds consumed, expired and unknown into one `null` (`src/auth/password-reset.ts:94-96,159-173`), so the page ships the two states it can prove — missing token and link no longer usable — and the three-way split is recorded as a follow-up requiring a new service outcome, outside this presentation-only card. (3) Self-service reset does not exist (`src/auth/admin-password-reset.ts` is administrator-initiated), so the forgot-password disclosure links to `/reset-password` and states truthfully that a valid administrator-issued link is required; it never promises a request form. (4) No literal color enters the diff: the strip and the six swatches reuse the generated `/application-assets/theme-swatch-<id>.svg` family exactly as `shell-theme-picker.tsx` does, and the storefront link targets `/store/<slug>`, never the template's `/pay/<slug>` shortcut.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** the auth card API does not cover a state-only panel, blocking F03 — mitigação: F02 delivers it with form and state children, and F03 reports `BLOCKED` instead of editing it.
- **Abortar se:** a criterion cannot be met without touching authentication, authorization, session or reset service logic — sinalizar `blocked: true` citando arquivo e linha exigidos.
