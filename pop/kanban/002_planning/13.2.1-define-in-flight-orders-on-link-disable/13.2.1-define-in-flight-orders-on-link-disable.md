# 13.2.1-define-in-flight-orders-on-link-disable — Define in-flight orders on link disable

- **Origem:** [[pop/roadmap/13-pre-production-hardening|Phase 13.2]]
- **Plano:** [[13.2.1-define-in-flight-orders-on-link-disable.plan]] · **Aprovação:** [[13.2.1-define-in-flight-orders-on-link-disable.approval]] · **Verificação:** [[13.2.1-define-in-flight-orders-on-link-disable.verify]]

## O quê

Specify in `pop/specs/checkout-and-order-lifecycle.md` (Open item: in-flight orders after an owner disables a reusable link) and implement what happens to `RESERVED`/`CREATING`/`PENDING` attempts and their `order_v2` rows when the owner deactivates a `REUSABLE` V2 link: capability reissue stops (already), the buyer status read reports an explicit state, provider orders keep reconciling to their authoritative outcome, and the owner sees the affected orders. No retry, no V1, no migration unless additive.

## Por quê

An undefined transition on live money state is a release blocker recorded in the spec's Open section since Epoch 4.
