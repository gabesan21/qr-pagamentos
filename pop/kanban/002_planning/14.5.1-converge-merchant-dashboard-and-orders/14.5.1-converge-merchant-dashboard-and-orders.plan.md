# Plano — [[14.5.1-converge-merchant-dashboard-and-orders]]

- **Etapa:** 002_planning · **Papel:** `pop-planner` (separado da execução)

## Objetivo e resultado esperado

- **Objetivo:** bring `/`, `/orders`, `/orders/[id]` and `/orders/v2/[id]` to the template's merchant commerce model — segmented period control, by-state/by-source chart, four inventory cards, live recent orders and leading products, storefront/first-run/error states, a provider-state filter and the template's columns, and an outcome editor plus comment composer that read like the template — without changing a database, route or DTO contract beyond the narrowest additive owner projection.
- **Resultado observável:** an owner switches the dashboard period without a full navigation, sees orders in period broken down by state and by source, opens a recent order or a leading product by clicking its row, filters the directory by provider state, records a local outcome through one select + note + confirmation, appends a comment beside their monogram and reads the thread newest-first, and jumps from an order to its payment link — meeting no BEM composition and no raw enum chip on these pages.

## Estratégia

Presentation plus the narrowest additive projection. No migration, no new route, no new mutation, no change to the `orders-v2` POST contract, no page-size/keyset/cursor change; every added read re-authorizes the owner and stays owner-scoped, and every amount keeps the exact-decimal path (never `Number`). Six decisions are pinned here so no executor re-decides them, each restated in the slice that implements it. **(1) The outcome editor is not gated on `confirmed`.** The template gate would delete a delivered capability: [[pop/specs/checkout-and-order-lifecycle|checkout-and-order-lifecycle]] pins `AD_HOC` orders as stateless, with the local outcome as their entire operator lifecycle, and allows a new append after a later reconciliation. The template's `in-progress`/`finalized` vocabulary has no counterpart either — the select offers exactly `LOCAL_FINALIZED` and `LOCAL_CANCELLED`, and a caption states the precedence rule. **(2) The directory gains `state`** (the eight provider states plus one explicit stateless option mapped to `state: null`) beside `source`/`money`/`from`/`to`/`link` — six registered filters. **(3) No `outcome` filter and no currency-pair filter:** the current outcome is the newest append of an append-only ledger, and "latest is X" is not expressible in the Prisma adapter without raw SQL or a denormalized column; registered filters are closed static enums while pair labels are dynamic registry rows, so `money` (`USD`/`FIAT`) stays. Both are recorded gaps, matching the admin decision of 14.4.2. **(4) No unified V1+V2 list:** V1 orders live in another table behind a byte-frozen service, so one keyset page cannot span both — the legacy section stays below the directory, restyled into the template's table look inside a merchant-local file. **(5) The period control is a merchant-local client wrapper**, not a promotion of the admin file: the visual contract already lives in the owned `Tabs` pill primitive, and moving an admin file just verified by 14.4.4 would falsify approved tests for no contract gain; a third consumer promotes it. **(6) Dictionary domains split by front** — new directory strings go to `orders-directory`, new detail strings to `orders`; already-filed keys are not moved. The added projection fields are counts and identifiers only: `ordersInPeriod` with `byProviderState`/`byOrigin` grouped over the owner's in-period orders, the order id on recent activity, the product id on best sellers, the owner's total link count and active/archived product counts. `isFirstRun` is derived, not read: no link and no recent activity. The error state is `error.tsx` with an inline strip whose retry calls `reset()`; a "stale" strip has no producer on a server-rendered page and is a recorded gap, as is the payment-data card, whose only candidate reference is the provider order UUID that never renders. Clickable rows reuse ids the owner already carries in their own catalog and order URLs; provider UUIDs, currency-pair UUIDs and line product UUIDs stay out. The comment thread renders newest-first through `Timeline`, which gains one additive optional `action` slot so the author-only CAS edit survives the restyle.

## Riscos e condições de aborto

- **Risco:** a extensão da projeção alarga o que sai da persistência, ou uma edição em view/primitivo compartilhado muda a superfície do admin — mitigação: só contagens e ids owner-próprios entram no DTO, `order-v2-views.tsx`/`order-views.tsx`/`timeline.tsx` mudam apenas por prop opcional ausente por padrão, e C11 confere os call sites do admin. Suítes merchant fixadas na BEM, nas duas `<details>` de outcome e nos comentários oldest-first quebram na integração: não são tocadas aqui e C13 as registra para 14.5.4.
- **Abortar se:** um controle do template exigir migração, mudança de DTO público, nova rota de mutação ou filtro que a gramática registrada não expressa — sinalizar `blocked: true` com o controle, o dado ausente e o arquivo consultado.
