## Contract boundaries

- Keep the four identity IDs and positive/reversed static variants closed; the manifest may add application-art roles but cannot remap identities or treat every SVG as a brand lockup.
- `public/brand/**`, `public/application-assets/**`, the manifest, and favicon are generated only. A second generation must reproduce every byte and reject undeclared files.
- SVG validation uses a closed element/attribute inventory; reject script, image, foreignObject, style/event handlers, external/data/network references, live text/font dependencies, malformed geometry, duplicate bytes, and unsupported color semantics.
- Bind each source and derivative separately: immutable source SHA/bytes/viewBox/parity ID, derivation kind, output SHA/MIME/intrinsic dimensions/modes/accessibility, and exact provenance/grant references.
- Favicon frames derive only from the new QR glyph and approved semantic palette; missing sizes or incoherent ICO directory/frame bytes fail.
- Six swatches retain exact stored IDs and validate their visual colors against 12.2.1's canonical values; do not branch identity/component code by theme.
- Application fallbacks and illustrations remain static presentation resources. They never create upload capability, media ownership, storage identifiers, quota, or lifecycle state.
