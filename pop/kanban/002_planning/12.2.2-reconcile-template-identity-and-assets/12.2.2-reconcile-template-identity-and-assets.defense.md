## Contestable decisions

| # | Decision | Adopted choice | Rejected alternative | Why | What would falsify it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity replacement | Replace canonical geometry/composition; keep four consumer IDs | Add a second template brand or compatibility adapter | One runtime identity prevents drift while avoiding route churn | A consumer needs a semantically distinct fifth application identity |
| 2 | Wordmark | Derive reviewed static Sora outlines from 12.2.1's pinned licensed bytes | Keep IBM Plex outlines or preserve template `<text>` | Template fidelity and static no-font contract both apply | Exact licensed Sora source cannot be reproduced or authorized |
| 3 | Source handling | Hash-bind immutable supplied SVGs, validate, and generate production derivatives | Serve `docs/template` or copy raw SVGs into `public` | Keeps reference immutable and runtime outputs closed/safe | Sanitization changes approved raster meaning beyond tolerance |
| 4 | Provenance statement | Record source commit and exact project-use grant without ownership claim | Label artwork project-owned/original | No LICENSE/NOTICE proves authorship or broad ownership | A signed upstream license establishes stronger terms |
| 5 | Asset ownership | Separate application artwork from merchant media | Store fallbacks/illustrations through `src/media` | Static app art has no owner/quota/lifecycle and must not imply merchant upload | Any supplied byte is actually user-uploadable owner data |
| 6 | Theme swatches | Validate against 12.2.1 token authority | Duplicate palette constants in brand code | Stable IDs need one visual source of truth | Swatches intentionally differ from canonical theme values |
| 7 | Visual proof | Dedicated asset contact sheet plus raster/semantic assertions | Migrate routes or edit `/design-system` now | Proves this task without taking later task ownership | Asset fidelity cannot be tested outside a production consumer |
