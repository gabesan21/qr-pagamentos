## Original request

Card What/Why: a separately rate-limited sessionless cart command that revalidates store, owner, products, quantities, prices, currency, limits, and totals before issuing one owner-derived one-time link, clearing only that store's cart and redirecting only after successful issuance — because browser carts are untrusted and must never choose payable facts or gain the merchant's creation authority. **Met.** `src/checkout/storefront-cart-checkout.ts:59-82` accepts exactly `{ items: [{ reference, quantity }] }` (any foreign key, custom-amount member, duplicate reference, or out-of-bound quantity is the one opaque invalid before any store work); `src/checkout/storefront-cart-checkout.ts:107-165` revalidates slug→owner (enabled storefront, `ACTIVE` merchant `USER` only), every reference (owner's active non-archived products), and the single active-mapped currency against rows locked `FOR SHARE` in one transaction; prices/titles/currency never come from the browser (they are not even read). Issuance reuses `src/auth/payment-link-v2.ts#create` server-side with a principal synthesized from the locked owner row; the client clears only `qr-pagamentos:storefront-cart:v1:<slug>` and redirects to `/pay/[identifier]` only on a validated `201`.
