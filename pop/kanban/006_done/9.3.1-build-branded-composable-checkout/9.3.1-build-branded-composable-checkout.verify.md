## Findings

### 1. BLOQUEANTE — the "two-column layout" of the card objective never renders; the grid collapses to one column at every width

- **Files:** `src/app/globals.css:1245` (`.checkout-v2__columns` → `grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, calc(var(--space-12) * 7)), 1fr))`) vs `src/app/globals.css:1231` (`.checkout-shell { max-width: var(--layout-max) }`), with `--reference-space-12: 3rem` (`globals.css:19`), `--reference-space-6: 1.5rem` (`globals.css:16`), `--reference-prose: 65ch` (`globals.css:25`).
- **Arithmetic:** minimum track = 3rem × 7 = **336px**; two tracks + gap = 336 + 336 + 24 = **696px**. The V2 page root carries `className="checkout-shell checkout-v2"` (`src/app/pay/[identifier]/public-checkout-v2-page.tsx:22`), so the grid container is capped at `--layout-max` = **65ch ≈ 520–650px** < 696px. `auto-fit` therefore always yields exactly one track, at 1440px included.
- **Evidence:** every 1440px capture in the run-bound artifacts renders the summary and the customer/payment card stacked in a single ~646px column — e.g. `artifacts/checkout/20260726230019/checkout-vault-blue-pt-BR-1440.png`, `checkout-pix-paper-en-1440.png`, `state-en-qr-copy-1440.png`, `state-en-confirmed-1440.png` (inspected visually by this critic). No capture in the 52-capture manifest shows two columns.
- **Why blocking:** the card's *What* (the original request, which outranks the plan) explicitly requires a "responsive two-column layout"; plan decision 5 pins "Two-column branded composition … Columns stack on mobile"; the F05 closeout in `DESIGN.md` now claims "the branded two-column composition … stacks its columns on narrow widths" — a claim the delivery contradicts. The two-column structure is dead code: it cannot be observed at any viewport.
- **Fix direction (004):** let the V2 page container exceed the prose cap at desktop widths (e.g. a wider `max-width` on `.checkout-v2` or a dedicated layout token), keeping the auto-fit stack on narrow widths; then re-run `checkout:evidence` so at least the 1440px captures demonstrably render two columns, and align `DESIGN.md`/spec wording with the observed behavior.
